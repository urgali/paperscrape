## 2. The police livery — VERIFIED, fixed

`police_stripe.svg` was a wedge: `polygon 0,4 68,0 68,10 0,13`, thirteen units tall and rising four
across its length. On the phone at 6× it read as a crooked band whose front end dipped into the
wheel arch and whose rear end rose past the sill — **the only livery in the set not aligned to the
car's own lines**. `taxi_checker`, which shares the same slot and the same blit, is a plain
horizontal rect and reads correctly.

Two horizontal candidates were rendered on the device and compared against the original:

| | footprint | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A, v4.16 | 68×13, tilted | crooked, into the wheel arch |
| **H9 — chosen** | **68×9, the chequer's own** | clean band on the doors, clear of both wheels |
| H11 | 68×11 | reaches lower, starts to graze the wheels |

Done through the asset pipeline: SVG rewritten, registry moved from 204×39 to 204×27, PNG rendered
from the source and copied into the runtime directory, `validate` and `compare` green.

## 3. The umbrellas — VERIFIED, and it was not an impression

Houses and parasols were generated by the **same call** — `generateSplitStaticCandidates`, same slot
arithmetic, same two depth bands, differing only in their seed. Slot `i` is `(i + 0.5) / n` for both
and the jitter is at most `0.35 / n`, so with `n = 10`:

- parasol `i` landed within **0.07 of a tile of house `i` — 76 px of 1080**;
- `depthFraction` came out **identical**, because it is derived from `i` and not from the seed.

A house's door is at its own centre. That is a mechanism, not a coincidence.

**The first fix was wrong and the test caught it.** Half a slot of phase leaves the two jitters
(±0.035 each) summing to more than the phase, and the worst pairing still closed to **0.0013 of a
tile**. `GroundPilesAndParasolsTest` measures the generated layout across every theme and rejected
it. A parasol is now placed *from its house*: one per house, `PARASOL_SIDE_OFFSET = 0.055` to one
side, alternating sides. The separation is a property of the placement instead of something the
jitter can undo.

## 4. Falling leaves — VERIFIED, fixed, before and after rendered

`drawFallingLeaves` ended every fall at one global `screenHeight * 0.88`, which is **below both
traffic lanes** (0.834 and 0.862). A leaf shed by a tree high on the hill drifted down across the
hillside, over the far lane and over the near one, and settled on the carriageway among the cars.

It now ends at `leafSourceGroundY[source]` — the tree's own ground line, which `recordLeafSource`
already received and simply did not store.

**Before and after were rendered at the same instant on the phone.** The changed pixels span
`y 1636..2100`, reaching the old floor at 2112 and past the near lane at 2068; in the before frame
there are leaves lying on the carriageway between and below the lane markings, and in the after
frame the road carries none.

A colour-based count was tried first and **rejected as contaminated**: the autumn canopies are
painted in the same four tones as the leaves, so 25 000 "leaf" pixels a frame were mostly crowns.
The before/after diff at a fixed instant is the measurement that isolates them.

## 5. Pumpkins — VERIFIED, resized

0.5 m, against a gift's 0.905, a bunny's 0.885 and an Easter egg's 1.00 — **half the size of an
egg**, and the only seasonal prop outside the family. On the phone, beside the gifts, snowmen and
penguins, it read as an orange bead.

Two device passes. The first compared 0.50 / 0.85 / 1.05 and chose 0.85; the maintainer reported it
still too small, so a second pass compared 0.85 / 1.00 / 1.10 in one frame with a gift, a snowman, a
penguin, a bunny and an Easter egg beside it.

| | verdict |
|---|---|
| 0.50 m (v4.16) | the runt; reads as a bead |
| 0.85 m (first pass) | better, still reads small beside the egg and the gift |
| **1.00 m — released** | level with the Easter egg, just under a gift; clearly a pumpkin |
| 1.10 m | stands in front of the penguin and hides it |

## 6. Halloween carves them — VERIFIED

`halloweenEnabled` already existed, is already per-theme, already persists and is already in the

## 7. Snow piles and Leaf piles — VERIFIED, new

Two per-theme sliders, 0–100%, **both defaulting to 0**, so a scene nobody has touched is exactly
the scene v4.16 drew. Snow piles appear only under the Winter palette and leaf piles only under
Autumn, and each slider is shown only under its own palette rather than greyed out under the other.

The scatter is `drawGroundFlowers`'s, salted differently: the same stratified hash — a slice of the
width each, depth free within the ground band — so a drift sits on its own ground line at its own
perspective, the set is identical every frame, and **nothing is allocated per frame**. The count is
`pileCount(density) = trunc(density × 18)`, which is what makes 0% draw *nothing* rather than one
lonely drift.

Verified on the phone at 0 / 50 / 100%:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| 0% | draws nothing at all |
| 50% / 100% | scattered drifts and heaps, on the open ground, sizes following perspective |
| on the carriageway | **zero pixels below the road's top edge, at 100%, for both** |
| on roofs or houses | none |
| grid-like | no — the scatter is hash-stratified, not uniform |

**Cost, measured on the device** rather than reasoned about: 40 rendered frames at each setting,
**0% = 63.79 ms/frame, 100% = 60.64 ms/frame**. The eighteen extra blits are inside the noise; the
100% run measured the faster of the two.

The sprites were **redrawn once** after the pipeline rejected the first pair: `validate` found the
declared content boxes did not match the PNGs and `normalize` found removable padding. Both now fill
their canvas exactly (`alpha bbox 0,0,108,21`), the blit origin moved with the canvas from -12 to
-7, and the pipeline is green.

## 10. Performance — VERIFIED on the OnePlus

The only new per-frame work is the piles, and both default to off. Measured directly rather than
inferred: **0% 63.79 ms/frame, 100% 60.64 ms/frame** over 40 rendered frames each, which is to say
the difference is inside the noise. No allocation, no state, no timer, no polling: the piles are N
sprite blits and nothing else, N ≤ 18.

Nothing else in this release adds per-frame work — the parasols are the same object count in
different places, the police stripe is the same blit of a different PNG, and the pumpkin is the same
blit at a different scale plus one more blit only while Halloween is on.

## 13. Limitations

- **21 of the 23 regenerated goldens are phone-authored without a cross-device measurement.** The
  emulator equivalence measured in v4.16 covered `traffic-day` and `traffic-night`; for the rest the
  two devices' agreement is inferred from the same ≥8 channel tolerance rather than measured,
  because this pass may not start an emulator. **INFERRED, not VERIFIED.**
- **The commercial buildings (bar, restaurant) were not audited on the device.** The capture
  harness's theme does not place them and building one that does was not worth the batch's time
  against the defects that were found. **NOT VERIFIED.**
- **Snow piles were not seen live under the Christmas theme**, only under Winter, which is the flag
  Christmas inherits. The gate is `winterColorsEnabled` and Christmas turns it on, so the behaviour
  follows; it was not looked at. **INFERRED.**
- **`assembleRelease` and `bundleRelease` are unsigned.** Signing needs the maintainer's keystore,
  deliberately not used.
- **No Compose UI test suite exists** (TST-03, an accepted project position). The two new sliders
  are covered by unit tests on the preference layer and by looking at the screen, not by a UI test.
- **The local repository has no `origin`**, so the tree could not be diffed against GitHub; the
  baseline comparison is against the v4.16 artefact delivered from here, file by file.
