# v4.25

- **The people are redrawn.** Everyone in the scene — the walkers on the pavement, the faces
  behind the shop windows, the driver and the passenger in every car — is drawn in the same
  language the trees and the sky were given in the last two releases: layered paper with a raised
  edge, separated by the paper's own shadow instead of by an outline. **They have faces now**: a
  suggestion of one, three marks at the size a person actually is on your screen, which is enough
  to tell a person from a shape without turning the scene into portraits. There are exactly as
  many of them as before, and nobody changed seat.
- **The people in the cars are facing the right way.** They were looking out of the back window.
  Now the driver and the passenger both look where the car is going, in both directions of
  traffic, and you can tell one from the other through the glass.
- **The children are children again.** A child in a car window used to be drawn at 98% of an
  adult, which is not what a child looks like. They are 90% now, which is the size the scene has
  always meant them to be.
- **The heads are heads.** The people behind a windscreen had come out narrow and a little low in
  the window — a face pressed in at the sides. They are drawn in the same proportion as everybody
  else in the scene now, they sit where a head sits, and there is still glass between the two of
  them. The busts fit their windows without ever poking through the roof, winter hats included.
- **The small figures stop shimmering as the scene scrolls.** Swipe between home screens and the
  distant people used to crawl and flicker along their edges: a sprite drawn at a fifth of its
  size loses whole rows of pixels, and which rows it loses changes with every pixel of movement.
  Everything small is now drawn from a copy made for the size it appears at, which removes about
  **half** of that shimmer and leaves the small figures visibly sharper. It also costs a little
  less battery than before rather than more.

**Nothing you have set changes.** No setting was added, removed or renumbered, no saved theme is
touched, and every scene you have built looks like itself — with the people in it drawn better.
