# PaperScrape v76.3

**Six fixes from the third pass on a real device.**

## Santa's reindeer are trotting again

The team stopped moving its legs when the whole group became a single drawing.
A drawing cannot bend, so the trot is a second drawing now, alternating with the
first — and the two reindeer step out of phase with each other rather than in
unison.

## Santa himself is cozier

Rounder coat, a fuller beard, rosy cheeks, a smile, a slouched hat with a bigger
pompom, and a mitten on the reins.

## Traffic behaves like traffic

Cars were bunching into packs. Two things caused it: every car in a lane ran at
its own speed, so the quick ones caught the slow ones and drove through them; and
every car snapped back to the same starting point at the end of its lap, which
threw away the head start it had over the car behind. Now a lane has one speed,
its cars are spaced evenly around the loop, and the spacing survives each lap.
The two lanes also sit further apart, so oncoming traffic reads as being on the
other side of the road.

## Birds are birds

They were reading as bats — a flat angular M with the wing tips pointing down.
They are gulls now: swept wings, a body, a head and a beak.

## The snowman's arms come out of his body

They were being placed at head height, so his twigs appeared to be stuck through
his face. They come out of the middle of his torso now, just below the scarf.

## Car windows sit properly in the cabin

The glass had no pillar behind it and a wide band of body in front. Moved forward
so there is a pillar at each end. The glass itself is unchanged.

## Still to come

The overall sizes of people, cars, houses and trees relative to each other are a
separate piece of work and are not touched here.
