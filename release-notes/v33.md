🐛 Fixed
- Seasonal decorations (added in the last update) now work the way you'd
  expect: Christmas keeps its snowmen and gifts by default, Easter keeps
  its bunnies and eggs, Tundra keeps its snowmen and penguins, New Year's
  Eve keeps its balloons, and Winter keeps its snowman — same as before.
- You can still turn any of these off, add ones that weren't there
  before (pumpkins at Christmas? snowmen on the beach?), recolor them,
  and save your changes over the built-in theme or as a new custom theme
  — exactly like houses, trees, and everything else in "Scene Objects".
