# v5.4

**The app's icon now changes with the season, everybody in the street has an umbrella when it
rains — children included — and two shops stand where the street always meant them to stand.**
This is a visible release: unlike v5.3, you will see the difference on your home screen and in
the wallpaper itself.

- **The launcher icon follows the time of year.** PaperScrape ships five seasonal drawings —
  spring, summer, autumn, winter and Christmas — plus the icon it has always had, and the app
  switches between them on its own as the date moves. You do not have to open anything: the
  wallpaper is already running, and it changes the icon the day the season turns.

  **It follows *your* calendar, not a fixed one.** The dates come from the same Holiday calendar
  screen you can already edit, so if you move the start of winter, the winter icon moves with it.
  Occasions that have no drawing of their own — Halloween, Easter, New Year — wear the icon of
  whichever season your calendar says covers that day. A fresh install wears the original icon
  until the first date check runs.

  Nothing blinks while this happens: your wallpaper is not restarted, and the app does not close.
  One thing is outside our control — some launchers keep their own copy of the icon on the home
  screen and are slow to notice it changed. If yours looks stale, a reboot settles it.

- **When it rains, the pedestrians put up umbrellas. All of them.** Until now this was a lottery:
  a hidden per-person roll decided who got one, and on some scenes it came up empty — it could
  rain on the whole street with not a single umbrella open. Now everybody walking carries one
  while the rain lasts, and everybody folds it away when the rain stops.

- **The children have umbrellas of their own.** They did not before, and could not: there was
  simply no drawing of a child holding anything — the adults' one would have floated well above
  their heads. A carrying pose was drawn for both kinds of child, in both their summer and winter
  clothes, so a child now walks under their own canopy, held at their own height, beside the
  adults.

- **The rain the umbrellas answer to is the real rain.** If you use Live Weather, the umbrellas
  open for the weather outside your window, not only for the rain a theme paints by hand. That
  was always the intention and it had never actually worked.

- **The restaurant and the bar are in the right place on the street.** Both buildings told the
  scene they were about twice as tall as they are drawn, and the layout believed them, so it
  walked them out of the way of trees and houses that were never in front of them. On eight of
  the twelve themes a shop now sits where it was meant to — on Sunset the restaurant comes in
  from the right-hand edge where there used to be sky. **The shops themselves are not redrawn:
  not one pixel of either building has changed.**

- **A repair you can only see by waiting.** One part of the night sky — the phase of the moon —
  was being read from your device's clock at the moment of drawing rather than from the scene, so
  the same night could paint a slightly different moon at two different hours. The moon the
  wallpaper draws is the real one, as it always was; what changed is that the drawing is now
  consistent with the rest of the picture.

Nothing you have set is affected. Your themes, colours, sliders and saved themes are exactly as
you left them, and there are no new settings.

**Still the case since v5.3:** this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators,
Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
