# PaperScrape v76.2

**Eight fixes to how the new artwork is placed and driven, all reported from a
device.**

None of these changes the drawings themselves. They change where each drawing is
put, which way round it faces, and how many of it are drawn.

## Cars and Santa were driving backwards

The redrawn cars and Santa's sleigh both face left in the new artwork, where the
old drawings faced right. Nothing told the code that, so every vehicle on the road
— and Santa — was mirrored and appeared to travel in reverse. They face the way
they are going again.

## Traffic has two lanes again

Every car was being put in the same narrow band, no matter which way it was
driving. So oncoming traffic drove straight through the cars coming the other way,
three or four vehicles could stack into what looked like a permanent pile-up, and
the dashed centre line sat above all of it with nothing on its far side.

There are two lanes now, and which lane a car is in decides which way it drives:
the near lane runs right, the far one runs left. The centre line sits between them
where it belongs.

## Windows back on the cars

The glass was hanging off the front of every car, over the bonnet and above the
roof line. It sits inside the cabin now, with the roof visible above it.

## Police markings back on the police car

The livery stripe was being drawn below the car and off to one side, so it looked
like a loose bar lying on the road — and left the white car itself completely
unmarked. The taxi's chequer had the same problem, one notch less obvious. Both
now run along the doors where they belong.

## One bird, not three

Birds were being drawn three at a time at a third of the size, from a
misunderstanding about the new bird drawing. There is one bird per bird again, at
the size it used to be.

## Snowman, penguin and bunny

Small parts were placed against the old drawings' proportions: the snowman's
carrot sat level with his hat brim and his scarf across his face, his twig arms
floated clear of his body, the penguin's beak sat on top of his head, and the
bunny's pink ear patches hung in the air beside her ears. All measured against the
new bodies and put back.

## Still to come

The overall sizes of people, cars, houses and trees relative to each other are
being worked on separately and are not touched here.
