This release is about Halloween, and about putting right many small things: raindrops with straight ends, heavy rain that runs smoothly, settings pages that fill the screen, switches that say why they are off, and a lighter app.

If you have 5.16 on Android 9 or 10: the update button in the app cannot install 5.17 there, because the fault is in the 5.16 that does the installing. Download 5.17 from the release page on GitHub and install it from there, once. From 5.17 on, updating from the app works on Android 9 and 10 too.

Halloween

- The Headless Horseman throws his pumpkins. He now throws the lit pumpkin he holds up: he swings his arm back and forward, and it leaves his hand, flies a little ahead and up, and falls on fire to the pavement, turning slowly, where it goes out. A new pumpkin then grows in his hand for the next throw. In 5.16 the pumpkins dropped from under the horse.

- Fewer Halloween decorations. No more bats. Each house and shop has one smaller cobweb, in a corner of its roof, and the jack-o'-lantern by every front door, as before. The cobweb is dark on light walls and light on dark ones, and turns from one to the other as the light fades in the evening and comes back in the morning.

- Moon stays in place. A new switch on the Halloween page: the Halloween moon stops crossing the sky and stays still in the middle, low over the roofs, under the clouds. Without Night sky by day it comes out after sunset and fades before sunrise. The Halloween theme as it comes has it on; every other theme has it off.

The rain and the sky

- Raindrops with straight ends. A drop is as long as before, with straight ends instead of round ones, and heavy rain is much lighter work for the phone: on our test phone it now runs smoothly where it used to skip now and then.

- The rain follows the clouds as they come and go. Turning the clouds off while it rains, or a forecast clearing, used to leave the rain under the vanishing clouds for a moment and then put it over the whole sky at once; now it spreads over the sky while the clouds fade, and narrows under them as they come back. Clouds coming into a clear sky now fade in, as every other cloud does. With the clouds at 0 %, rain and snow now fall on the whole sky, as with the clouds off (they used to stop).

- Live Weather. With Open-Meteo, real rain and snow are now drawn at their real strength (they were drawn about four times lighter). The forecast is still asked for every hour, and no longer each time the phone's position moves a little: only a move of more than about 11 km asks for a new one at once, so after a shorter move the new weather can take up to an hour. The phone's position is no longer looked up every hour for a provider whose key is missing. A request the provider refuses waits for the next hourly try instead of being repeated within minutes, and Weather & time says the provider refused it rather than that it could not be reached. When a provider says its limit of requests has been reached for now (for example WeatherAPI.com's monthly allowance), Weather & time now says so and Live Weather stays on, instead of saying that the key was not accepted or that the provider could not be reached. A wrong Open-Meteo key now shows Live Weather off, with a line that says the key was not accepted, as for the other providers.

- Sunrise and sunset after a time-zone change, and the moon at the change of the clocks. After a flight into another time zone, sunrise and sunset are right again; near the polar circles, a day or a night of less than an hour no longer makes the sky jump; the moon no longer jumps when the clocks change.

The street

- The police lights rise slowly at dusk instead of switching on at once.
- An umbrella now comes in with its person at the edge of the screen instead of appearing out of nothing.
- Spring's flowers, and the piles of snow and leaves, are on the whole street, not only on one of its two screens.
- Falling leaves no longer come from firs or from Halloween's bare branches and withered palms, and no longer start again all at once as the street scrolls round.
- Saved themes show their seasonal decorations once, as the theme they were saved from, even the ones you saved with 5.16.

The settings

- The pages fill the screen on Android 8 to 14 (there was an empty band at the top and the bottom).
- They open on your own theme. They could first show the factory theme, Sunset, for a moment.
- A switch that cannot do anything shows off, and says why. For example, on a random or saved theme whose street has no trees, Show Trees, Lights on the trees and Christmas firs are off and greyed, with "This theme has no trees."; the same for houses, towers, parasols, shops and public buildings, and for the Christmas firs where the palms stand in for the trees. Your choice stays saved and comes back by itself when something can show it.
- The theme changes at midnight with the holiday calendar even while the wallpaper is on screen, and the holiday calendar page shows the new day's holiday after midnight. Settings left open past midnight show and change the new day's theme.
- Check for updates when I open PaperScrape now checks each time you open the settings, not when you come back to them from a page they opened (the browser, the file picker, the installer).
- A theme's name is now at most 100 characters, typed or in a theme file you import.
- The city search stops when you change or clear the name, and searching the same city again shows its results.
- If an app is missing (a browser, a file picker, the installer), the settings say so in a short sentence instead of closing.
- Backups. A backup taken while the settings or the saved themes cannot be read for a moment is no longer saved with the factory settings, or without your themes, in it: the file you chose stays empty, and the settings say so. Restoring one at such a moment is refused the same way. A backup now also carries the saved themes this version cannot read, and restoring one no longer deletes those on the phone.
- The colour picker reads your touch right after the phone is turned, and the Seasons & decorations row counts only the decorations that show.

Fewer interruptions

- Turning the phone's location off while the wallpaper was asking for it closed the wallpaper on Android 8 to 10; it no longer does. With many objects on the street the falling leaves could close it too; no longer.
- The wallpaper switches to its slower drawing only after the graphics fail several times in a row, not after four failures days apart; and with little memory it no longer throws away its pictures only to load them again.
- Turning on bold text, plugging in a keyboard or changing the SIM no longer cuts an update download short; importing a file from a slow place, such as a cloud drive, no longer freezes the settings; and if the settings cannot be read for a moment, the wallpaper reads them again within a minute instead of staying on the factory settings until it restarts.

Smaller

- About 310 KB smaller to download, with the same pictures. On a phone set to a language other than English, a few of the libraries' own words (for example "Autofill" in a text field's menu) now show in English, as the rest of the app does, and TalkBack reads a few labels in English.

The work for your phone

- On our test phone the wallpaper uses about the same share of the processor as 5.16, or a little less, with about the same memory: from about 1 % to 3 % less on an autumn noon and night, a winter night with snow falling, a Christmas night with everything on and the Halloween theme at night and at noon, and about the same at a Halloween dusk. In heavy rain it uses about a fifth less, and a frame comes a moment late about one time in 75 instead of one in 7.

If you are updating from 5.16:

- In the Halloween theme, Moon stays in place is on, even if you had changed or saved that theme. A copy of the Halloween theme you saved under its own name, and every other theme, have it off.
- Raindrops have straight ends; heavy rain runs more smoothly.
- A switch that cannot show anything now reads off, with a line that says why: on a random or saved theme that has none of something; on any theme where its amount is too low for one to stand, or people at 0 % by day and by night; and Christmas firs on Beach and Desert, where the palms take the trees' places. Your choices are kept.
- With the clouds at 0 % and rain or snow on, it now falls on the whole sky.
- A theme you saved under its own name shows the seasonal decorations of the theme it was saved from, once and in the same places: it may show fewer of them than before, or in other places.

Nothing else you have set has changed: your themes, colours and sliders are as you left them.

Still the case since v5.3: this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators, Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
