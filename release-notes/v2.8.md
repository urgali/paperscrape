# v2.8

**A pass over the buildings, after looking at them next to a person.**

**Shops are the right size again.** v2.7 made the bar and the restaurant taller by scaling the
whole drawing, which also gave them four-metre doors and five-metre signs — bigger than the
buildings they were on. Both now have a proper first floor with windows above the shop front, so
they stand a little taller than the largest house because they *are* a storey taller, not because
their doors grew. Every door in the scene is now about the height of a door.

**Small houses match the big ones.** They were slightly too tall and their windows sat higher and
larger than the large house's. Same window size now, same sill height, a lower ridge and a wider
front — the small house reads as the compact version of the same house rather than a different
building.

**The tower is calmer and has a front door.** It was eighteen storeys of tiny windows squeezed
into a very tall block. It is shorter now, with fewer and larger windows on plausible floors, and
a proper glazed entrance at street level with an awning over it — standing on the same ground the
people walk on.

**Christmas trees.** With the Christmas lights switch on, about one tree in three is now a
decorated fir: snow on its branches, lights, and presents at the foot. Which trees become firs is
fixed for a given scene, so the wood does not reshuffle itself while you watch.

**Christmas lights are spread out.** They used to light the three lowest floors of a tower in one
block. They are now scattered over the whole facade, and over the windows of houses and shops
too — different for each building, and steady.

Nothing else changed: the world scale, the pedestrians, the birds, the dolphins, Spring, Halloween,
autumn leaves and the flowers are all as they were in v2.7.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with v1.0, so
Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears your saved settings
and custom themes.
