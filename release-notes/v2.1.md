# v2.1

**Nothing on screen has changed.** This release contains no new features, no fixes to
the wallpaper itself, and no changes to the artwork, the themes or the settings. If
v2.0 is running the way you like it, v2.1 will look and behave identically.

What changed is the offline tooling that checks the artwork against its sources, and
the documentation that reports on it.

Three checks in that tooling had been failing since the artwork was redrawn, and were
being carried as a known limitation. They turned out to be describing sprites that no
longer exist in the form the checks assumed — a flower planter and a road marking that
were both plain rounded rectangles before the redesign and are neither of those things
now. The checks were re-derived against the artwork that actually ships.

With that out of the way it became possible to measure the thing the failures had been
obscuring: how closely the shipped artwork matches the drawings it was made from. Every
one of the 118 sprites matches in shape exactly. The only differences are in how the
edges of curves are smoothed, and none of them are large enough to move an edge across a
pixel — far below anything visible on a phone.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with
v1.0, so Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears
your saved settings and custom themes.
