# v76.12

- **Snow settles on buildings.** Houses, the restaurant, the bar and the tower all
  get their own snow in the winter and Christmas themes — a real layer cut to each
  roof's shape, resting on it, with chimneys and the tower's mast standing out of the
  drift.
- **People can be turned off or thinned out.** A new People screen under Scene
  Objects, with a show switch and a density slider. Their seasonal clothing works
  exactly as before.
- **Police cars and fire engines no longer carry passengers.** They're crewed
  vehicles; ordinary cars and taxis still carry someone in the back.
- **A prettier, cheaper night sky.** Most stars are now points with a few bright
  sparkles among them, which reads more like a real sky than seventy identical
  twinkles — and costs about a third as much to draw.
- **Boats and dolphins share the whole lake again** without landing on top of each
  other.
