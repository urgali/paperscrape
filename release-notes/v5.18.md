This release is about the street: the whole scene stands higher, and under the road the street is seen a second time, nearer, with its own shops. It also brings trees to every theme, rain and snow only under clouds, and a forecast that is asked for again as soon as the wallpaper comes back on screen after an hour.

If you have 5.16 on Android 9 or 10: the update button in the app cannot install 5.18 there, because the fault is in the 5.16 that does the installing. Download 5.18 from the release page on GitHub and install it from there, once. From 5.17 on, updating from the app works on Android 9 and 10 too.

The street

- The scene stands higher. The hills, the houses, the road, the people, the cars, the lake, the mountains, the rainbow and the birds are all a little higher on the screen, and the sun and the moon rise from a little higher too. The clouds, Santa's sleigh, the Headless Horseman, the stars and the Halloween moon that stays in place are where they were.

- A second row below the road. Where there was empty meadow under the road, the street is seen a second time, nearer and a little bigger: its houses, trees, palms, parasols and seasonal decorations, a pavement of people walking in front, flowers and piles of snow and leaves on the meadow, and leaves falling from its trees. In two places along the street the restaurant, the bar and the shop take turns, with their colours, opening hours, people going in and out, Christmas lights and Halloween pumpkins; the school, the fire and police stations and the towers stay on the street behind. The row follows the switches and amounts you already have; there is no new switch. Random and saved themes have it too. The theme cards in the gallery and the strip on World & scene do not show it. The line under Show people now says that people walk on both sides of the road.

Trees on every theme

- Trees can now be turned on on every theme. A random, saved or imported theme whose street has no trees used to have Show Trees greyed, with "This theme has no trees."; now it is given trees of its own, in the theme's colours, and the Density slider on the Trees page thins them. Show Trees is on as themes come, so a random theme born without trees shows them at once; a theme you saved with the trees off keeps them off until you turn Show Trees on. A tree that would stand with its trunk across the front of a shop or another building among the houses is left out.

Rain, snow and clouds

- No rain and no snow without clouds. Without Live Weather, with Show Clouds off or # of Clouds at 0 %, nothing falls, no umbrella opens and no lightning flashes. Show Rain/Snow then reads off and greyed, with "No rain or snow without clouds. Turn Show Clouds on and raise # of Clouds above 0%; your choice is kept until then.", and Thunderstorm says that it needs clouds. Your choice stays saved and comes back by itself with the clouds. When the clouds go, the rain stops with the last of them, in about a second and a half. The Rain and snow row of World & scene reads "Off - no clouds", and a theme's card in the gallery shows no rain or snow without clouds either.

- When Live Weather's forecast says rain or snow under a clear sky, a few clouds are drawn, about a fifth of the sky, and it falls under them.

Live Weather

- When the wallpaper comes back on screen and the last forecast is an hour old or more, a new one is asked for at once, even after earlier tries failed. If the phone's network is not ready yet, it is asked again a few seconds later. A request the provider refused still waits for the hour, and nothing is asked while the wallpaper is not on screen. The line under Live Weather now says the weather is refreshed at most once an hour while PaperScrape is on screen.

- An unexpected error can no longer stop the weather until the wallpaper restarts: the next try runs as planned.

Building

- The tools that build the app now use Guava 33.7.2, which closes a security advisory. Guava is not part of the app itself.

The work for your phone

- On our test phone the wallpaper uses a little more of the processor than 5.17, for the second row of the street below the road: about 3 points of one core more, from 6 % to 9 % more depending on the scene (an autumn noon and night, a Christmas night with everything on, Big City at noon, a Halloween night in heavy rain), with about the same memory. On the Christmas night with everything on a frame comes a moment late about one time in 16 instead of one in 28, and in the Halloween heavy rain one time in 41 instead of one in 58; in the other scenes about as often as before.

If you are updating from 5.17:

- The scene stands higher, with the second row of the street below the road.
- If you had rain or snow on with the clouds off or at 0 %, nothing falls now, and Show Rain/Snow is greyed with its line; your choice comes back by itself when you turn the clouds on.
- A random or saved theme without trees now has them: shown if Show Trees was on, hidden if it was off.

Nothing else you have set has changed: your themes, colours and sliders are as you left them, and the cars keep their colours.

Still the case since v5.3: this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators, Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
