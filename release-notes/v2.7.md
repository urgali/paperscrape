# v2.7

**Two bugs the device pass found.** Snow on a small house sat crooked — the drift was still cut
for the narrower roof the house had before v2.6 widened it, so it fell short of both eaves. And
autumn leaves appeared out of clear sky: they were spawning at one fixed height straight across
the screen rather than off the trees. Every leaf now leaves a crown that is actually on screen,
drifts across it and falls from there.

**Flowers.** A new switch: wildflowers scattered over the open ground, three kinds at three
sizes, larger near the road and smaller at the back. On by default in Spring, available on any
theme. On or off is the whole setting — the flowers keep their own colours, because a meadow that
followed a building tint would be a meadow of the wrong flowers.

**Christmas lights on the buildings.** The lights switch now also hangs a string under every
window: both windows of a small house, all four of a large one, the restaurant and bar frontages,
and the three lowest floors of a tower. Real strings with a slack cord, not dots beside the glass.

**Balloons are gone.** Not hidden — removed, along with their sprites, their toggle and their
settings entry.

**Shops and towers are bigger than houses.** A restaurant used to be shorter than a cottage, so a
parade of shops read as outbuildings behind the houses. The hierarchy now runs house → shop →
tower, as it should.

**Skyscraper windows are centred.** The grid was flush against the left edge of the facade with
the last column cut off. It sits square on the front now.

**Release downloads are named after the version** — `PaperScrape-v2.7.apk` rather than
`app-release.apk`.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with v1.0, so
Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears your saved
settings and custom themes.
