# v2.0

Every built-in theme has been reviewed and now presents a scene that holds together.

The themes shared far more of their settings than they should have, and several
shipped combinations nobody would have chosen on purpose.

**Winter, Christmas, New Year and Tundra are winter again.** Snow settles on rooftops
and trees, and people dress for the cold. All of this already existed — it was simply
never switched on for the themes that needed it, so the winter scenes had green summer
trees, bare roofs, and people in short sleeves standing on snow. Winter and Christmas
now snow by default, too.

**Winter and Christmas are two separate things.** Christmas lights used to come with
the snow, so a plain snowy January was impossible and Christmas always cost a full
winter presentation. They are now independent: you can have snow without lights, lights
without snow, both, or neither. Winter is the season; Christmas is the decoration.

**New Year keeps its own character** — fireworks, balloons, a night sky — while sharing
the season's snow. It is not a second Christmas.

**Autumn is autumn.** The leaves turn and drift down, and pumpkins appear, instead of a
summer wood standing under an autumn sky.

**Other fixes across the set:**

- Beach umbrellas no longer stand in the snow. They stay where they belong.
- The Beach stands on sand. Its ground was drawing in the sea's own colour.
- The Tundra's lake has lost its sailboats and its dolphins.
- The Desert has palms instead of broadleaf woodland.
- The City is built rather than settled: many more buildings, far fewer cottages.
- The Tundra has a scattering of trees rather than a forest.

Every one of these is a default, so a theme you have already customised keeps your
choices. "Reset everything to defaults" on the Seasonal Decorations screen now really
does restore the theme's own defaults, rather than switching everything off.

**Note for anyone running a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1
with v1.0, so Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which
clears your saved settings and custom themes.
