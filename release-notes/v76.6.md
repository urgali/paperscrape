# v76.6

The final balance pass on the rebuilt scene, from a look at it running on a real
phone.

- **People are bigger.** They were readable and not much more; now they have
  presence in the scene.
- **Cars are slightly smaller**, for a more natural size next to a person.
- **The road is narrower.** It read as a wide dark band with the traffic sitting
  inside it; now the two lanes are sized to the cars on them, and the road no
  longer dominates the scene vertically. It still never covers the houses behind
  it, and the traffic is unchanged.
- **Tall buildings are a little shorter**, so they stay a backdrop instead of
  competing with the foreground.
- **Trees have more presence** beside the houses.
- **Boats and dolphins are noticeably bigger** and readable at a glance.
- **Presents are bigger** — they were nearly specks.
- **Snowmen are visible on snow.** A white snowman on white ground had almost
  nothing separating him from the background. He now has a soft tonal edge, still
  white and paper-like, no cartoon outline.

Everything else is unchanged: the hills and mountains, the artwork, the depth,
the traffic behaviour and every setting.
