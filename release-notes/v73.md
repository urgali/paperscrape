🏠 Look and feel — houses, now in two sizes
- Houses now come in a small and a large variant, picked randomly per house. Both got a cozier
  redesign: a flower planter under a window, a warm little porch light by the door, and softer
  chimney smoke. The chimney sits to the side of the roof, like a real one, not dead-center.

🚗 Look and feel — cars redrawn
- Cars are now a single smooth, low silhouette instead of looking like two stacked boxes.
- Fire trucks now actually look like fire trucks: the ladder sits properly on the roof, with a
  pair of warning lights next to it — it wasn't reading as anything more than a plain red car
  before.

🏪 Look and feel — shops now identifiable at a glance
- The restaurant has a hanging fork-and-knife sign; the bar has a hanging beer-mug sign. Both
  used to look like generic buildings with no way to tell what they were.
- The skyscraper now has a stepped top tier and a rooftop antenna, so it reads as a tall
  building rather than a plain rectangle.

🌳 🎈 Look and feel — tree, hot air balloon
- The tree canopy is now a fuller, rounder cluster so it reads clearly as a tree instead of a
  bush or cloud.
- The "balloon" is now a proper hot air balloon (envelope + basket) instead of a party balloon
  on a string.

☀️ 🐦 🎅 Look and feel — sky
- The sun now has a defined sunburst behind it.
- Birds got a softer, rounder wing shape — the old one had sharp points that read as bats to a
  few people, this one reads as a bird at a glance.
- Santa's sleigh now uses the newer, more detailed artwork (fluffier beard, gift-filled sack,
  gold trim) instead of the plainer shapes it had before.

🎃 Look and feel — seasonal decorations
- Snowman, gift, penguin, Easter egg, bunny, and pumpkin all got the same sprite-based redraw
  the rest of the scene already went through, for a more consistent look and a lighter load on
  the CPU/battery than the old shape-by-shape drawing.

🚶 New — people
- People now walk along the sidewalk, dressed for the season (summer or winter/holiday outfit).
  You'll also spot someone at a house window now and then, and a driver behind the wheel of
  passing cars.

🐛 Fixes from this round's on-device testing
- Cars were drifting outside the road strip — a coordinate mismatch introduced by the car
  redraw above; the road now correctly contains the whole car again.
- Driver heads in cars were oversized, nearly covering the whole car — resized down.
- Walking people were rendering tiny compared to everything else in the scene — they were
  missing the same scale factor every other object already uses.
- The sun could look like it had a second, paler disc next to it (easy to mistake for a second
  sun or the moon) — simplified the new sunburst art to remove the effect.
- Removed a few unnecessary per-frame allocations in the new people-drawing code that could add
  avoidable CPU/battery load.

⚙️ Under the hood
- Every new/changed sprite in this update was rendered and visually checked against a test
  background before shipping, not just eyeballed from its source code — caught and fixed a
  handful of clipped or misaligned sprites (car body, tree canopy, dolphin, house door/window
  overlap) that wouldn't have been obvious without actually looking at the pixels.
- Full local test build passed (Gradle `assembleDebug`) before this release — a first for this
  project's aesthetic-pass updates, previous rounds had no Android build tools available in the
  working environment.
