# v2.9

**The settings rebuilt, and previews that show what a theme actually looks like.**

**Settings are five screens instead of one long list.** Everything used to live in a single
scroll about four and a half screens tall, with the weather settings sitting between the clock and
the parallax slider and the version number at the bottom of the same list. There are now five
places: the home screen tells you which theme is showing and who chose it, and **Weather & time**,
**Seasons & decorations**, **World & scene** and **Advanced & about** hold the rest. Nothing was
removed; every setting from v2.8 is still there.

**You can see which theme you are looking at.** The preview on the home screen carries the theme's
name, and a *Picked by date* badge when the calendar chose it rather than you. In the theme
gallery, the card the calendar picked is badged *Today*, your own selection is outlined, and a
built-in theme you have edited is marked *Customised* — so "selected" and "showing right now" stop
being the same word.

**Theme previews are real scenes now.** A gallery card used to be a sky gradient with a circle for
the sun and a band for the hills, which made half the themes look alike. Each card now draws a
small version of that theme's own world: Christmas has lit firs, presents and snow on the roofs;
Halloween has the orange carved moon over bare branches and pumpkins; Beach has the sea with boats
and dolphins; Tundra has penguins among sparse conifers; the desert has dunes and palms; City has a
skyline. They show only what the theme actually contains — if a theme has no lake, its preview has
no lake.

**Weather has a section of its own.** It used to disappear entirely when you switched the sun off
real time, with nothing saying where it went. Location is now one choice — off, your phone, or a
place you type in — instead of two switches that turned each other off, and Live Weather stays
visible with the reason when it cannot run.

**Seasonal decorations are grouped by season.** Winter, Christmas, Halloween, Easter and Spring
each hold their own switches, so the pumpkins are with Halloween and the flowers are with Spring
rather than under the Christmas heading. Autumn and winter tree colours are one choice — none,
autumn or winter — which is what they always were underneath. Each decoration's density and
colours are one tap away instead of expanded on the page, which turned that screen into about
sixty controls in one scroll.

**The app looks like itself.** The settings UI had four of Material's roughly thirty colour roles
filled in, so switches, sliders and dialogs fell back to a default violet. The whole scheme is now
built from PaperScrape's own cream and orange, in light and dark. The emoji that marked sections
are gone, replaced by proper icons.

**Nothing about the wallpaper changed.** The renderer, the scene, the artwork, the themes, the
calendar, Live Weather, your custom themes and every default are exactly as they were in v2.8.
This release is the settings screen and the previews.
