✨ New
- New "Sun and Moon" section: show/hide and pick your own color for
  each, plus an option to turn off realistic moon phases in favor of a
  simple full moon.
- New "Sky" section: full control over the sky's colors — day, night,
  and dedicated sunrise/sunset colors near the horizon.
- New "Stars" section: show/hide and how many appear.
- New "Clouds" section: show/hide, how many, and their color — drifting
  slowly across the sky.

All per theme, like everything else in Scene Objects — freely editable
and saveable over the built-in theme or as your own custom one.
