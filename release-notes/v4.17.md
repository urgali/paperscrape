# PaperScrape v4.17

An art pass over the things lying on the ground, two new seasonal sliders, and three small things
that turned out to be quietly wrong once somebody looked at the scene on a phone instead of at the
numbers.

## Snow piles and leaf piles

Two new sliders, 0 to 100%, one for winter and one for autumn. Winter scatters small drifts of
settled snow over the open ground; autumn scatters heaps of fallen leaves. Each appears only under
its own seasonal palette, each is remembered per theme, and both start at zero — so nothing changes
until you move one.

The leaf heaps are separate from the leaves drifting down off the trees. You can have either, both
or neither.

## Halloween carves the pumpkins

The Halloween switch already stripped the trees to bare branches. It now also turns every pumpkin
into a jack-o'-lantern — two triangular eyes and a grin, carved into the pumpkin that was already
there. Turn it off and they go back to being pumpkins.

The pumpkins are also bigger. They were half the size of an Easter egg, which is not a size a
pumpkin comes in; they now sit alongside the gifts and the snowmen instead of hiding behind them.

## Three fixes

**Umbrellas were almost always standing in a doorway.** Not bad luck: houses and umbrellas were
placed by the same piece of code, on the same grid, at the same depth, so each umbrella landed
within about seventy pixels of a house's centre — which is exactly where the door is. Each house now
gets its umbrella beside it, on alternating sides.

**Falling leaves settled on the road.** Every leaf fell to the same fixed height, below both traffic
lanes, whatever tree it came off — so leaves from a tree up on the hillside drifted all the way down
and lay on the carriageway among the cars. A leaf now lands at the foot of its own tree.

**The police car's stripe was crooked.** It was drawn as a wedge that tilted up along the car,
dipping into the front wheel arch at one end and rising past the window sill at the other — the only
livery in the set that did not follow the car's own lines. It is now a straight band on the doors,
in exactly the place the taxi's chequer sits.

## Nothing else moved

The cars, the road, the buildings, the people and the way the occupants sit in the windows are all
unchanged. Everything in this release was compared before and after on a real phone.
