# v76.8

Two technical fixes. Nothing about the scene's appearance changed.

- **Saved custom themes are repaired on load.** A theme saved on an earlier build
  could pull the road back to where it used to be, on top of the ground the people
  walk on. Where the road is, how fast each lane moves and which way it drives are
  now worked out fresh every time a theme is loaded, so a saved theme can no longer
  carry a road that no longer exists. Your themes keep what is actually yours: how
  many cars, their colours and their types.
- **The asset tooling can see the scene renderer again.** An internal check had
  silently stopped inspecting most of the artwork more than a year ago. It works
  again, and the stale measurements it turned up have been corrected.
