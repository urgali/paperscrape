# v4.19

- **Three cars instead of one.** The traffic now carries a compact, a saloon and an estate
  instead of the same shape over and over. Each vehicle keeps its own model for as long as it is
  on the road — it will not change while it drives past you, while you swipe between home
  screens, or when another car comes into view.
- **Children ride.** The passenger seat used to be adults only, because the old cabin could not
  hold a child's wider shoulders without pressing their head against the window frame. The three
  new cabins were drawn around the widest of them, so a boy or a girl can sit up front now — and
  the two people in a car are never the same person twice.
- **The estate is properly long.** It gained length at both ends, in front and behind, and it now
  reads as the big one of the three at a glance. It has a third window over the load bay and rails
  on the roof.
- **The fire engine was redrawn to match.** It stood next to the new cars with hard corners and
  small wheels poking out from under a flat floor — the older drawing, next to the newer one. It
  now shares their language: rounded arches with air around the tyres, the same corner radii, and
  for the first time a red lamp at the back, so which way it is going is readable from the engine
  itself.
- **The police car's beacons sit on their own lamps again.** They had been glowing a little to the
  right of the light bar's domes since the previous release.
- Every car's shadow is now the length of that car rather than one shared size.
