⚙️ Under the hood
- Houses, buildings, trees, and palm trees now use pre-rendered images instead of being redrawn
  from scratch every single frame — the same approach classic paper-cutout live wallpapers use. This should
  ease CPU/battery usage, especially in scenes with lots of these objects. All your color
  customization still works exactly the same.
- This is the first batch — cars, clouds, mountains, the lake, and everything else are still on
  the old approach for now and will be converted in future updates.

No visual changes intended in this update; if anything looks different or off, let me know.
