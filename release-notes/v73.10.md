## Lighter on the processor

Nothing about the wallpaper looks different. Every scene, colour, animation and
setting is exactly as it was — this release is about how much work the app does
behind each frame.

Several parts of the drawing code were rebuilding the same fixed information from
scratch thirty times a second: lists of colours and positions that never change,
a clock reading that only changes once a minute, and settings applied to the
brush over and over with the same values. All of that now happens once, or once a
minute, instead of once per frame.

The result is less work and less memory churn per frame, which should show up as
lower processor use rather than as anything you can see. Rain, snow, clouds, the
Christmas lights on the trees and the water all draw precisely as before.

This is the first step of a wider look at where the drawing time goes. The larger
items found along the way are recorded and will be worked through carefully, one
at a time, with the wallpaper checked on a real device before anything that could
change its appearance is accepted.
