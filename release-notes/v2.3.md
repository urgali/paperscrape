# v2.3

Four changes you can see.

**Halloween.** A new switch that carves the moon into a jack-o'-lantern and strips every
tree down to bare branches. It is its own switch: turning it on does not turn winter or
Christmas on, and turning either of those on does not turn it off. Any combination works.

**Horror Sky.** A second, separate switch: near-black overhead with a hard orange band at
the horizon. Separate from Halloween on purpose, so you can have bare trees under an
ordinary night sky, or an ordinary scene under a lurid one, or both, or neither.

**The dolphins now splash.** They used to simply vanish at the surface on the way back
down. A short burst of water now appears at the moment and place each one re-enters, sized
to the animal that made it, so a far dolphin throws a small splash and a near one a bigger.
The dolphins themselves were also redrawn — a proper beak, a swept dorsal fin and a notched
tail, instead of the rounded shape they had.

**The birds look like birds.** They were reading as bats: the wings had a sharp elbow and a
broken leading edge, and the head sat apart from the body. They are gulls now — smooth
tapered wings sweeping back to a point, a body and head in one piece, a wedge of tail.

Nothing else in the scene changed. Your saved themes and custom colours are untouched, and
a theme saved before this release simply loads with both new switches off.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with
v1.0, so Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears
your saved settings and custom themes.
