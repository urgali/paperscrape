# PaperScrape v4.13

The automatic night colours are properly dark now, and the asset toolchain's long-standing "the
fingerprint doesn't match" warning turned out to be measuring the wrong thing.

## Night colours actually look like night

The "Day sets night" and "Night sets day" switches added in v4.12 were working, but the colours they
produced were too light. Snow-white hills on the Christmas theme became a pale grey that still read
as daytime; a bright red house became a red that was barely darker.

The rule behind them has been rebuilt. It now works in a colour space that matches how the eye
judges brightness, and halves it, so:

- white becomes a **cool dark grey-blue** rather than a mid grey;
- a bright red becomes a **deep red**, still obviously red;
- very dark colours stay dark without collapsing to black, and nothing turns grey.

Checked on a real phone rather than by eye on a chart: the Christmas hills go from near-white to a
proper night tone, and red houses to a deep red, in both directions of the switch.

Colours you set by hand are untouched — "Both" behaves exactly as it did.

## Under the bonnet

The tool that checks the sprite-rendering pipeline had been reporting a mismatch for several
releases while every single sprite came out identical. The cause was found: it was hashing the
*compressed* PNG, so it changed when the compression library changed, not when the drawing changed.
It now checks the pixels. The whole asset test suite passes for the first time in months.

The unit tests also now notice when a sprite image changes, which they previously did not.

Nothing else in the scene moved, and no settings were renamed or removed.
