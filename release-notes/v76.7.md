# v76.7

The last balance pass on the rebuilt scene.

- **People now walk between the village and the road**, on the strip of ground
  that belongs to them, instead of along the bottom edge of the tarmac. They are
  smaller than before because they are standing further away — the same rule that
  sizes everything else in the scene.
- **The road sits slightly lower.** It is exactly as wide as it was; it only
  moved, to make room for the ground the people now walk on.
- **Christmas lights stay in the tree.** They were hanging out of the bottom of
  the canopy and over the trunk. They now sit inside the leaves and sway with the
  branches.
- **Parasols are bigger**, back in proportion with the houses and people around
  them.

Dolphins, sailboats, the hills and mountains, the artwork and every setting are
unchanged.
