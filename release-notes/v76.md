# PaperScrape v76

**Every drawing in the wallpaper has been redrawn.**

This is the largest change to how PaperScrape looks since it was first built. The
scene itself is untouched — the same objects in the same places, the same
animations, the same settings and themes — but the artwork they are made of has
been drawn again from scratch, as layered paper cutouts with proper colours and
cleaner shapes.

## What you will notice

Everything reads as paper more clearly. Adjacent layers now differ enough in tone
to stay separate at the size they are actually drawn on screen, where before they
could collapse into one flat shape.

**Winter faces exist.** People walking along the pavement always changed with the
season, but the faces at house windows and behind car windscreens did not — they
looked the same in January as in July. They now have hats, scarves, hoods, raised
collars and cold cheeks.

**Houses light up properly at night.** Windows switch on with a warm lamp glow
instead of the glass slowly changing colour, and tall buildings now show a lit
facade after dark rather than a grid of small squares.

**The hot-air balloon's basket is no longer white.** Neither is anything else that
was drawing blank.

**New things to see.** Palm trees have a fuller frond fan on a wider trunk, trees
have a proper bark trunk instead of a plain rectangle, thunderstorms now show an
actual bolt of lightning rather than only a flash, and fireworks and rainbows have
been redrawn as artwork.

Birds now fly in small groups of three rather than as one wide shape, and Santa's
sleigh sits where the presents it drops actually come from.

## What changed that you might miss, and why

A few colour controls no longer act where they used to, and this is deliberate.

The sun, the star sparkles and palm fronds are now finished drawings with their
own colours, rather than plain shapes the wallpaper colours in as it draws them.
That is what lets them carry shading and detail — but it also means **Sun Color**
now tints the glow around the sun rather than the sun itself, palm fronds no
longer follow **Fall Colors**, and star colour follows the artwork. Everything you
could recolour that is still a plain shape — houses, buildings, tree canopies,
cars, clouds, the moon, and every decoration's body — works exactly as before.

Similarly, all tall buildings now light the same windows at night rather than each
picking its own, and fireworks are a single colour scheme rather than five.

If any of this reads wrong on your screen, it is worth reporting: the fix belongs
in the drawings.

## Under the hood

The wallpaper now carries slightly less artwork than before — 14.4 MB against
16.1 MB — despite having three more drawings in it, mostly because Santa's sleigh
was stored at more than six times the resolution it is ever drawn at.

Every drawing in the app now has a source file it can be re-made from. Until this
release only 22 of them did, which is why several fixes over the past year had to
work around the artwork instead of correcting it.
