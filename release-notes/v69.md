🐬 Look and feel — lake decorations redrawn
- Dolphins and sailboats on the lake have been redrawn from scratch in the same "paper cutout"
  style as everything else so far — a rounder, friendlier dolphin shape with a visible eye and
  belly stripe, and a sailboat with a proper hull and a big triangular sail with a little
  pennant flag.

⛰️ Look and feel — mountains and hills get some shading
- Mountains and hills used to be a single flat color each — they now have a subtle two-tone
  "paper fold" look (a touch lighter on one side, a touch darker on the other for mountains; a
  soft light-to-base gradient near the ridge line for hills), matching the softer shaded look
  the rest of the scene already has.
- Unlike houses/buildings/cars in previous updates, these two didn't get converted to sprite
  images — turns out the original app draws mountains and hills with plain colored shapes too
  (no image files involved), so there was nothing to "convert" there. Instead they got this
  shading touch-up to look a little less flat.

⚙️ Under the hood
- This is "batch 4, part 1" of the larger aesthetic overhaul (the "terrain" group — mountains,
  hills, dolphins, sailboats). Still remaining for future updates: clouds, birds, sun/moon,
  stars, rainbow, Santa's sleigh, and all the seasonal decorations (snowman, gift, penguin,
