# v1.0

The first release of PaperScrape.

A paper-cutout landscape that lives on your home screen: hills, a lake, a village and
a road, with the sun and moon following the time of day and the scene scrolling as you
swipe between screens.

**What's in it**

- **Ten themes**, from sunset and beach to winter, Christmas and Easter — plus custom
  themes you build yourself and keep.
- **Every part of the scene is yours to change.** Houses, buildings, trees, umbrellas,
  cars, people, hills, mountains, clouds, stars, the lake and its boats and dolphins:
  each can be shown, hidden, thinned out and recoloured, day and night separately.
- **Seasonal decorations** — snowmen, presents, pumpkins, Easter eggs, penguins,
  rabbits, hot-air balloons, Santa's sleigh — on any theme, any time of year, not just
  the "right" one.
- **Real weather, if you want it.** Live Weather replaces a theme's manual rain, snow
  and cloud with the conditions outside your window. If your location isn't available
  it says so and falls back to the theme's own weather.
- **The sun and moon follow your day**, from your device clock, your location, or a
  place you pick yourself.
- **A scene with real depth.** Everything from a rabbit to a tower is drawn at a size
  the scene works out from how far away it stands, so the whole landscape holds
  together as you look across it.

**Note for anyone who has been running a test build:** version numbers restart at 1
with this release, so Android will not install it over an older one. You will need to
uninstall first, which clears your saved settings and custom themes.
