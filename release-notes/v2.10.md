# v2.10

**Find your city by name, and reach the bottom of every settings screen.**

**The last setting on a screen is no longer half-hidden.** Scrolling to the bottom of a settings
screen left the final row — its text, its switch, or its button — partly under the gesture bar on
some screens and not others. Every screen now leaves the same clearance below its last row,
measured from what the system actually reserves, so the bottom of a page is reachable and readable
everywhere.

**Custom location: type a city name instead of coordinates.** Setting the location for Live Weather
used to mean knowing your latitude and longitude. There is now a search field: type "Milano", pick
it from the list, done — the coordinates are filled in for you and the place is shown by name.

**You choose which one you meant.** If several places share a name, all of them are listed with
their region and country, and nothing is selected for you:

    Springfield — Illinois, Sangamon, United States
    Springfield — Massachusetts, Hampden, United States
    Springfield — Queensland, Australia

Your current location stays exactly as it is until you tap one.

**Coordinates still work.** The latitude and longitude fields are still there, under the search, for
anyone who wants them — and the coordinates of a place found by search are shown too, so you can
check them.

**If the search can't reach the network** it says so and offers to try again, and your existing
location is left untouched. A search that finds nothing says that instead, which is a different
answer from a search that failed.

Nothing else changed: Live Weather itself, the weather cache and its fallback, phone location, the
automatic theme, the themes, the previews and the wallpaper are all as they were in v2.9.
