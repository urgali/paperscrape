
# v5.1

**The palms are redrawn, the holidays are yours to move, and the app has a new icon.** Four changes,
none of them behind a setting you have to find first.

- **The palm trees are a new drawing.** The old ones were five stiff triangles on a pale stick. The
  new palm is a coconut palm: seven broad blades that arch up and fall again, three coconuts under
  them, and a trunk that curves and leans over a flared foot with old leaf scars up the front. It is
  cut paper like everything else in the scene now — every blade has its own shadow falling on the
  one behind it — and the trunk finally stands out against the sand instead of disappearing into it.

  Its winter and Halloween versions were redrawn with it. The frosted palm is a whole frosted crown
  rather than white tips dabbed on the green one, and the Halloween palm's blades collapse and fold
  back down the trunk.

- **And the palms go dark at night now.** They did not. Every other thing in the scene dims as the
  evening comes on, and the palms stood in full midday green under a midnight sky — near enough to
  glowing, once you noticed it. They now lose their light along with the trees around them, and by
  the same amount, so if you have edited your tree colours the palms follow what you chose.

- **You can turn the palms off.** Seasons & decorations → Summer → Palms. With it off, the Beach and
  the Desert draw the same trees every other theme does — in the same places and the same number, so
  the shore does not go bare. On by default, and on every other theme it changes nothing, because no
  other theme has palms to begin with.

- **The holiday calendar is yours.** Settings → Holiday calendar. Every seasonal window the app uses
  — when winter starts, when the autumn palette comes in, when Halloween and Christmas run — can be
  moved to the dates you keep, and reset one at a time or all at once. It travels with your backup.

- **Flowers know what season it is.** The wildflowers on the open ground come in two kinds now: in
  bloom under an ordinary or a spring palette, and gone over to seed heads and thistles under an
  autumn or a winter one. A meadow in full flower under falling leaves was the one thing the ground
  never got right.

- **A new launcher icon.** A row of the town's own buildings, drawn in the same cut paper as the
  wallpaper, so the icon on your home screen looks like the thing it opens.

Nothing you have set is affected. Your themes, colours, sliders and saved themes are exactly as you
left them, and a Beach or Desert theme you saved before this update keeps its palms.
