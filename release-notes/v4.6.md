# PaperScrape v4.6

The people you can see through a windscreen, and three things that were only ever going to be
found by looking.

## The people in the cars were the wrong size

A pedestrian's head is **31% of their own height** — 25 of the 80.67 local units a walk sprite
draws. That is a paper-cutout proportion, and it is the one the whole scene is drawn in.

The busts behind glass were not drawn in it. They were sized to fit inside the window *with their
shoulders*, which put a driver's head at 0.320 m against a pedestrian's 0.547 m — 59% — on a plane
that is **nearer the viewer than the pavement**. The people inside the cars read as children.

v4.3 fixed the other half of this same report and fixed it correctly: pedestrians were 8.6% too
tall and the cars beside them looked like toys. Nobody then checked the occupants, and the
arithmetic that would have caught it did not exist, because the size table has no entry for a head.

Three things had to move together, and none of them is a number anybody chose:

- the glass is drawn **19 local units** tall instead of the 16 the artwork is authored at, growing
  downward into the door to exactly the line where the police stripe and the taxi chequer begin;
- a bust's content is **exactly as tall as the glass it sits behind**, standing on the sill — one
  rule replacing three separately tuned scales, for the driver, the passenger and the fire engine's
  own cab;
- nothing else. `CAR_METRES_TALL` is unchanged, the lanes are unchanged, the projection is
  unchanged, and a car measures the same number of pixels as it did in v4.5.

Measured on rendered 1080x2400 frames, the same scene on both builds: a near-lane driver's face
went from 12 px to 15 px, a far-lane driver's from 10 to 13, a passenger's from 10 to 13. The
nearest pedestrian's face is 15 px in both.

## A pedestrian could paint over a car

`drawPeople` ran after the vehicle loop, and every pavement row is behind every traffic lane —
0.795 and 0.807 against 0.834 and 0.862 — so a walking figure drawn on top of a car was always
wrong. It stayed invisible because the two have to coincide horizontally before anything shows.
The deepest figure the generator can produce sits **24 px of a 2400 px screen below a far-lane
car's roof**, 32 against a police light bar, and painted its shoes across the roof whenever a car
passed behind it.

The people are now drawn before the traffic. Nothing else changed: no road geometry, no pavement
line, no vehicle renderer.

## Restoring a backup left the settings screen showing the old values

Not a backup defect. Both stores were written correctly and immediately; the screen simply never
repainted.

`SceneTheme.equals` compares by `id` alone — deliberately, because the class holds `IntArray`s and
an id is what every lookup means by "the same theme". But `CustomThemeEntry` is a data class
containing one, so a restored set of themes with the same ids and completely different contents is
`==` to what was there before, and `collectAsState`'s default equality policy decides nothing has
changed.

The settings screen now holds its saved themes in a state that treats every emission as news, and
updates the theme registry from the same collector so the two cannot disagree about which is
current. `SceneTheme.equals` is untouched.

## An interrupted restore could leave half of one

Backup import writes two DataStores in order and rolls the first back if the second throws. It ran
on the settings screen's own coroutine scope, which Compose cancels when the screen goes away — so
a rotation or a back press between the two writes left the preferences new and the themes old, and
then skipped the rollback as well. The two writes and the rollback are now uncancellable. No
journal, no new store, no format change.

## Background location: nothing added, on purpose

A live wallpaper keeps drawing with the app closed and the screen off, and its forecast depends on
where the phone is. The obvious reading says that costs either "Allow all the time" or a foreground
service with a permanent notification.

Measured on a Pixel 9 / Android 17 emulator with the settings screen never opened since boot and
only "while in use" granted: neither. The active-wallpaper binding puts the process in a state that
carries foreground-location capability with the screen on *and* off, a GPS fix was delivered, and
the weather refreshed. So v4.6 adds no permission and no service, and adds tests that will fail if
a later release adds one without saying why.

**Not verified:** the Network provider. The emulator has no network location provider to test
against, so the runtime evidence covers GPS only.
