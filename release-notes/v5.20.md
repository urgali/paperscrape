This release gives the row below the road room to breathe, puts the right icons in the menus, gives the shops and the public buildings a switch each on their own pages, and lets Live Weather fully own the clouds and the rain while it is on.

If you have 5.16 on Android 9 or 10: the update button in the app cannot install 5.20 there, because the fault is in the 5.16 that does the installing. Download 5.20 from the release page on GitHub and install it from there, once. From 5.17 on, updating from the app works on Android 9 and 10 too.

Below the road

- The row below the road now shows the street once instead of twice, with about one shop or public building to a screen instead of two or three, taking turns as before. A house mostly hidden behind another one is left out. So more of the meadow shows between the houses: on Autumn about one shop or public building, one house and two trees to a screen.

The menus

- Icons that showed the wrong thing now show their own: Parasols a beach umbrella, Rainbow an arc, Birds the swallow that flies in the scene, Holiday calendar a month, Source a cloud with an arrow, Export app backup an arrow into a tray, Import app backup an open file.

Shops, public buildings and towers

- The switch Show shops and public buildings on the Towers & offices page is replaced by two: Show Shops, at the top of the Shops page, and Show Public buildings, at the top of the Public buildings page. Each switches its own buildings among the houses, below the road too, and on the theme's card. Switched off, their turns along the street stay empty.

- Show Buildings on the Towers & offices page is now Show Towers, and holds only the towers and offices.

Live Weather

- While Live Weather is on, the Clouds and Rain and snow pages leave the clouds and the rain to it: instead of their controls, a line says what the scene is drawing at that moment, the real weather, or this theme's own weather while the first forecast is awaited or none is in effect. The colours of the clouds, the rain and the snow stay yours.

- When you switch Live Weather off, the scene goes back to this theme's own weather at once. When you change the Source, the key of the provider in use, or the Location, it drops the old weather at once and shows this theme's own weather until a forecast for the new choice arrives. Moving a custom location does not drop it: the new place's weather follows. Typing the key of a provider you are not using leaves the weather on screen as it is.

- The rainbow no longer shows while the real weather brings rain, snow or a thunderstorm. When the real weather takes it away or brings it back, it fades out or in over a second and a half, as the clouds do, instead of vanishing and appearing at once. Show Rainbow and its opacity still act at once.

Building

- The tools that build the app are up to date (Android Gradle Plugin 9.5.1, Gradle 9.9.1); nothing in the app itself changes for it.

The work for your phone

- On our test phone the wallpaper uses a little less of the processor than 5.19: 0.6 to 1.4 points of one core less, 1 % to 3 % less, on an autumn noon and night, a Christmas night with everything on, Big City at noon and a Halloween night in heavy rain, with about the same memory. A frame comes a moment late less often: on the Christmas night with everything on about one time in 14 instead of one in 11, and in the Halloween heavy rain one in 33 instead of one in 22.

If you are updating from 5.19:

- Below the road you see the street once, with more meadow.
- The shops and public buildings stay as they were: if you had Show Buildings or Show shops and public buildings off, they stay off, and their switches are now on their own pages. Turning Show Towers back on brings back only the towers.
- With Live Weather on, the Clouds and Rain and snow pages show a line instead of their controls, and the rainbow leaves while the real weather rains, snows or storms.

Nothing else you have set has changed: your themes, colours and sliders are as you left them.

Still the case since v5.3: this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators, Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
