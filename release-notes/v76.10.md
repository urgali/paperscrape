# v76.10

- **Live Weather now works with a custom location.** Turning it on, and later
  changing your location, both update the scene straight away — no restart, no theme
  change, no waiting. Two separate problems were behind this: the switch could be
  read before it had been recorded, and changing your location did not tell the app
  the weather it was showing was for the wrong place.
- **The Easter rabbit and eggs are bigger.** At their real-life sizes they were a
  few pixels against ground of much the same colour, and effectively invisible.
