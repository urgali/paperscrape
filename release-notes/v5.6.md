# v5.6

**There is a school on the street now, only children look out of its windows, and every vehicle
in the scene has been redrawn.** This is a release you will see: a building that was not there
before, and six vehicles rebuilt from scratch in the paper language the rest of the scene has
been using for a while.

- **A school, the sixth kind of building.** Until now the street was built from small houses,
  large houses, towers, the restaurant and the bar. The school joins them: a wide two-storey body
  with a clock turret over the entrance, a flag on the roof, a porch light over the door, four
  tall windows along the ground floor, and a cream plaque on the facade with a red pencil on it.
  It stands on every theme, so whichever one you use, it is there.

  The plaque carries a picture and not a name, deliberately: at the size this building is drawn on
  a phone, a written word would be a smudge. It is the same device the street already uses — the
  bar's sign is a beer mug and the restaurant's a round mark, neither of them a word.

- **Only children stand at a school's window.** Every other building fills its windows from the
  whole cast, adults and children alike. A school does not: an adult behind a school window is a
  thing that cannot happen any more. Two or three of the four ground-floor windows are occupied at
  a time, and it is only the age that changes: the face and the skin at that window are the ones
  that would have been there anyway, one age younger.

- **All sixteen vehicle drawings have been redone.** The three cars, the taxi, the police car and
  the fire engine — bodies, windows, headlamps and tail lamps, the taxi's roof sign and chequer,
  the police stripe and light bar, the fire engine's ladder. They were the last things in the
  scene still drawn in the older style, and they now match it: the wheels sit **in front of** the
  body rather than inside a cut-out arch, the glass is a separate sheet of paper laid into its
  opening, and each part is a cut shape with its own shadow underneath, the way the buildings and
  the people already were.

  They are also smaller than what they replace: the new set of sixteen takes less room in the
  download than the sixteen it removes, which paid for part of the school.

- **A side effect you may spot: the bar stands a little nearer the front.** The street reserves a
  stretch of depth for shopfronts, and it used to be split in two — restaurant at the back half,
  bar at the front. It is split in three now, one part each for the restaurant, the school and the
  bar, in that order from far to near. The restaurant has not moved. The bar has, on every theme,
  by about one step along the street. Neither building has been redrawn.

Nothing you have set is affected. Your themes, colours, sliders and saved themes are exactly as
you left them, and there are no new settings.

**One thing about saved themes.** A theme you saved before this version keeps the buildings it
was saved with, and that set does not include a school — the positions of its buildings were
frozen when you saved it. The twelve built-in themes all have one. Saving a fresh theme from a
built-in one gives you a school in it.

**Still the case since v5.3:** this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators,
Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
