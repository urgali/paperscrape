# v3.2

**The tests that guard the picture now run by themselves, the part of the app that actually draws
your wallpaper is under test for the first time, and the sun sets on the right day.**

A maintenance release. Nothing new to look at, and nothing you use works differently — except in the
two places where it was wrong.

---

## The sun no longer sets "at midnight" when it sets after it

Where you are inside your timezone decides what time the sun is overhead, and it is rarely exactly
noon. Put a long summer day on top of a late solar noon and the sun genuinely sets after midnight:
that is an ordinary June in Iceland, in northern Alaska, on any island keeping a timezone far from
its geography.

PaperScrape used to force both times back inside the same calendar day, which does not move such a
day — it deletes whichever end did not fit. Sunset came out as "exactly midnight", and in the worst
cases a place with twelve hours of daylight was given a day zero hours long and sat in permanent
night.

A day is now allowed to cross midnight, and the scene follows it: the sun stays up past 00:00 where
it really does, and comes up before it where it really does. Ordinary places are completely
unaffected — the same scene, at the same times, as before.

## Searching for a place can no longer hang

Asking Android for the name of a place can fail, and when it did, PaperScrape was not listening. The
row kept saying "Locating..." for as long as you left the screen open, because the app was waiting
for an answer that had already been given up on.

It now always finishes: with a name if there is one, with your coordinates if there is not, and
after a few seconds at most either way. On older phones the lookup also no longer happens on the
thread that draws the screen.

## Tidying

The README described a bird you could summon by tapping the wallpaper. That gesture was removed
some releases ago; the line is gone, and so is the leftover request for the system to keep sending
touch events to a wallpaper that ignored them.

## Behind the scenes

Two pieces of test infrastructure, invisible in the app but the reason future releases are safer:

- **The reference-image tests now run automatically** on every change, on an emulator, instead of
  only when someone remembered to run them by hand. Twenty-four checks: fourteen reference frames of
  the scene, three of the graphics backend, and the settings-file recovery suite from v3.1.
- **The graphics backend that actually draws your wallpaper is under test.** PaperScrape draws
  through hardware acceleration where the phone supports it, and that path — around seven hundred
  lines of it — had nothing checking its output at all. It is now compared, frame by frame, both
  against its own reference images and against what the software path draws, so the two can no
  longer drift apart unnoticed.
