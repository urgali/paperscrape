# v3.8

**A third weather service to choose from, and a lot of new checking that nothing else moved.**

Nothing about how the wallpaper looks has changed. If you have never opened the weather settings,
this release does nothing you can see.

---

## OpenWeather, as a third option

Live Weather can now fetch from **OpenWeather** as well as Open-Meteo and WeatherAPI.com.

**Open-Meteo is still the default and still needs no account.** The other two are there for people
who would rather use a service they already have, or who need one whose free terms allow commercial
use. A free OpenWeather account needs an email address and **no payment card**, and gives 60 lookups
a minute — far more than the once-an-hour refresh uses. Your key is stored only on your device and
sent only to OpenWeather.

A note on the provider picker: with three services in it, "WeatherAPI.com" no longer fitted on one
line and its label spilled over the buttons either side of it. It now reads "WeatherAPI" in the
picker and keeps its full name everywhere else.

## The app now tests its own traffic

This one is invisible but worth explaining, because it is the kind of gap that hides real bugs.

PaperScrape checks its drawing by rendering reference pictures and comparing them pixel by pixel.
There were seventeen of them, and **not one contained a car** — cars start off-screen and drive in
over the following seconds, while each reference picture was a single instant captured before any of
them had arrived. So the whole of the traffic system, every car, every lane, every size, was
unchecked.

There are now two reference pictures taken thirteen seconds in, with four vehicles on the road in
both. To be sure they are worth having, six deliberate faults were introduced — a car moved by a
single frame, the cars switched off, half of them removed, the cars made ten per cent taller, a lane
shifted, the speed changed — and all six are caught. None of the seventeen existing pictures changed
by so much as a pixel.

## A correction to the last release

v3.8 was going to fix the snow on winter trees: the previous release reported that the wallpaper
placed the snow cap slightly off-centre on the treetop.

**That report was wrong, and this release retracts it rather than acting on it.** Measured properly
this time — by overlaying the two pieces of artwork and counting — every one of the snow cap's
17,182 pixels already lands on the treetop. The earlier conclusion came from comparing the sizes of
the image files instead of the drawings inside them. "Correcting" it would have pushed a few hundred
pixels of snow off the tree and into the sky.

So nothing moved, and there is now a test that checks the snow sits on the tree, so the same wrong
conclusion cannot be reached twice.
