✨ New
- New "🎃 Seasonal Decorations" menu, separate from "Scene Objects": snowmen,
  gifts, balloons, penguins, Easter bunnies, Easter eggs, and pumpkins can
  now be turned on and off, colored however you like, and used on ANY
  theme — not just their "traditional" one. Want pumpkins at Christmas or
  snowmen on the beach? Now you can.
- All of the above now have full color customization (they used to be
  fixed colors you couldn't change).
- Brand new pumpkin decoration.

⚠️ Changed
- Since these decorations are now opt-in everywhere, existing themes
  (Christmas, Easter, Tundra, New Year's Eve, Winter) no longer show
  their old "default" snowmen/gifts/bunnies/balloons automatically —
  head to the new Seasonal Decorations menu to turn the ones you want
  back on.

🔧 Under the hood
- Continued build-system maintenance.
