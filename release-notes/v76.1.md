# PaperScrape v76.1

**Four fixes to the new artwork, all reported from a device.**

## The moon was cut off on one side

The crescent moon had a straight vertical edge slicing down its right-hand side.
The drawing ran off the edge of its own canvas and was simply chopped there.
Redrawn so the lit edge follows the moon's own rim; every phase is a clean disc
again. The gibbous moon had the opposite version of the same mistake — it was
drawing a thin crescent instead of a nearly-full moon, with one crater floating
outside it — and is fixed in the same change.

## Snow now covers the whole treetop

The white cap sat inside the crown rather than on it, leaving a green rim above
the snow and bare green corners at both shoulders. The cap is cut to this
canopy's actual outline now, so the snow reaches the ridge and both shoulders and
falls away with an uneven lower edge, the way settled snow does.

## Drivers sit in the window again

The head visible through a car's glass was landing a third of the way down the
door, below the window entirely. It was being positioned as though it were the
old, smaller drawing. The head itself is unchanged — only where it is placed.

## The fire truck is a fire truck

It was a red car with a ladder floating above the roof. It now has its own
vehicle: a taller boxy body, a separate cab with its own window, a cream stripe
over three equipment lockers, a dark chassis, and the ladder carried properly on
the roof rack rather than hovering over it. Its outline can no longer be confused
with the taxi or the police car.
