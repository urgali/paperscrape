# PaperScrape v76.4

**Seven fixes from the fourth pass on a device.**

## The road looks like a road

The strip was reaching far further above the top lane than below the bottom one,
so one lane looked like a road and the other like a verge, and the top edge rode
up over the ground the houses stand on. Each lane now owns half the strip and the
dashed line sits exactly between them.

## Windows sit in the middle of their walls

The large house's four windows were pushed to one side, leaving a wide blank wall
on the other. They are centred on the door now, and the small house's window and
door mirror each other about the middle of the wall.

## Mountains are one colour again

Each mountain was drawn as two halves, one lightened and one darkened, which read
as two different mountains meeting at a hard vertical seam straight down the peak.
One colour per mountain; the only division left is the one the hills make by
overlapping them.

## Dolphins breach instead of gliding

They were drawn all the time, sliding across the water permanently visible. They
now come out of the water, arc over, nose down and disappear. The drawing itself
was redone too — it was reading as a shark.

## Sailboats are one boat

The sail was off to one side of the hull and drawn on top of it, so the two pieces
looked like separate objects floating together. The mast now steps into the deck
amidships, with the hull in front of the sail's foot.

## Live Weather applies straight away

Turning it on did nothing visible until the wallpaper was restarted or a theme was
changed: the check ran on its own timer and then only once an hour. The switch now
starts the fetch immediately.

## Cars carry passengers

Every car had exactly one person in it. A car may now carry a passenger as well —
another adult, a boy or a girl — sitting in the rear side window, on the other side
of the pillar from the driver. **The driver is always an adult**; children only
ever appear as passengers.

## Not fixed in this release

Snow does not settle on house, shop or tower roofs in winter. The V2 asset set has
no snow artwork for them — the trees have their own snow cap and that is what makes
them work — so this needs new drawings rather than a placement change, and it is
better done deliberately than improvised.

The overall sizes of people, cars, houses and trees relative to each other remain a
separate piece of work.
