# v4.4 — rain you can see, and the rest of the traffic back

`versionCode = 35`, `versionName = "4.4"`. Baseline: v4.3 (`versionCode = 34`).

Two defects, both of them "something that is switched on is not on the screen".

---

## 1. Rain is drawn at the size it was designed to be, on every screen

**Reported as** rain not showing at all with Location off, Live Weather off, Clouds
on and Rain on — while snow, in exactly the same state, worked.

**Reproduced before anything was changed**, and it is not a state bug. Rain and snow
share one code path; nothing anywhere decides that one falls and the other does not.
Both were reaching the frame, and both were being drawn in **absolute canvas pixels**:
a 2 px stroke, a 16–26 px streak, a 2–4.5 px flake. Every other layer in the scene is
sized against `SceneSpace`'s viewport scale — down to the dashes painted on the road —
and precipitation was the one that never adopted it.

The numbers had been tuned at the 360×800 frame the tests render, not at the 2400 px
reference the rest of the scene uses. Measured on the same scene at two viewport sizes:

| viewport | rain | snow |
|---|---|---|
| 360×800 | 0.94% of the frame | 0.65% |
| 1080×2424 | **0.11%** | **0.08%** |

So on a phone the whole effect was about a ninth of its intended size. Snow lived
through that on contrast alone — a white disc still reads against a blue sky. Rain, a
translucent pale-blue hairline against a pale-blue sky, did not. That is the whole of
"snow works and rain does not".

**The fix.** Every precipitation size is now stated at the reference height and
multiplied by the viewport scale, exactly as the road markings already were. On a
1080×2424 screen rain went from 0.11% of the frame to **0.72%**.

**Nothing else moved.** The constants are the old ones times three and the test frame
is a third of the reference height, so 360×800 renders byte for byte what it did
before and **not one golden changed**.

## 2. A theme saved with the traffic turned down gets the rest of its cars back

v4.3 stopped saving a *thinned* car list into a theme, and put the cars back into any
built-in theme that had been saved with **none**. It did not put them back into one
saved with **some** — a theme saved at 50% kept six cars for ever, at 20% one car.

Those keep a road, which is why they were not what the original report was about, but
they are damaged in the same way: the missing cars can never come back, because raising
the density filters a list they are no longer in. Thinned to a single car the theme also
loses one of its two lanes, and the road is painted from what is left.

They are repaired now, and only when it can be **proved** rather than guessed: the app
rebuilds what the old save path would have written, from the theme's own car list and
the density stored in the theme itself, and puts the cars back only if that reproduces
what is on disk exactly. Anything it cannot account for is left alone.

As before, the repair writes the car list and nothing else. Your density, your
visibility switch, your colours, the theme's name and everything else are untouched, and
it happens on load — no gallery visit, no button, and a backup restored from an older
version is covered too.
