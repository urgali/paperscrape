# v2.5

**The world is bigger.** Everything standing on the ground — houses, buildings, trees, people,
cars — is about an eighth larger. The scene read as a model of a place rather than a place; the
proportions between things are untouched, so nothing has moved relative to anything else.

**Things stop disappearing into the background.** A pale house on pale ground, a white gull over
a white cloud, snow on a bright roof: those were separated by nothing at all. Sprites now carry a
very fine tonal edge, in their own colour rather than a black outline, tucked inside the shape so
nothing changed size.

**Small houses look like houses.** They had one window and a door pushed to one side, which read
as a cabin. The door is now central with a window on each side, and the house is a little taller.

**Halloween reaches the palms.** Beach palms kept their green fans while every other tree went
bare. They now have a dead crown of their own.

**A more orange moon.** The carved face is unchanged; the disc behind it is now a warm lantern
built from concentric paper rings rather than a flat white circle.

**A new Spring theme** — the twelfth. Pale washed sky, sharp new green on the hills, full canopies.
Not Easter, and not the beach.

**Automatic theme by date now covers the whole year.** It used to change the theme in December, at
Easter and over the summer, and leave your manual choice alone the rest of the time. The full year:
New Year 1–7 January, Winter to 1 March, Spring to 31 May, Beach through August, Autumn in
September, Halloween for all of October, Autumn again in November, Christmas to 26 December, New
Year to the 31st. Easter moves with the calendar and covers Good Friday to Easter Monday, taking
precedence wherever it lands. Changes happen at your local midnight.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with v1.0, so
Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears your saved settings
and custom themes.
