# v5.5

**Every theme now shows the whole cast of houses, towers and cars instead of the same few over and
over, and the sky no longer jumps when you wake the screen.** Both of these are repairs: nothing
new has been drawn for this release, and what you will notice is drawings you already had that
were never reaching the street.

- **The street is no longer built out of two or three houses repeated.** PaperScrape draws houses
  from ten different silhouettes — two sizes, one or two storeys, and a gable, mansard or turret
  roof. Which ones a theme used was decided by a hash, one house at a time, and hashes cluster:
  on the worst theme the whole scrollable width was built from **two** silhouettes, and the typical
  theme used four. Now each theme deals its houses out of the catalogue, so it shows as many
  different ones as it has room for — typically **seven**, and never fewer than three.

  **Four of the ten houses were never drawn at all.** Not rarely: never, on any theme, at any
  density, in any version of the app that has shipped. They were in the download and unreachable —
  a rounding error in the maths that chose them had quietly fenced them off. They are on the street
  now, which is the part of this release you are most likely to actually see.

- **Towers and traffic got the same repair.** Every theme now shows both tower crowns rather than
  only one, and all four vehicle types — the plain car, the taxi, the police car and the fire
  engine — rather than two or three of them. The proportions are unchanged: special vehicles are
  still occasional, they are just no longer missing altogether from a theme.

- **A side effect you may spot: some shops stand somewhere else.** The layout keeps shopfronts
  clear of whatever would hide them, and it measures that against the houses beside them. Different
  houses, different neighbours — so on some themes a shop has shifted along the street. Every
  shopfront is still as visible as the rule requires, and no building has been redrawn.

- **The sky no longer snaps when you turn the screen back on.** With Live Weather on, the forecast
  is refreshed once an hour, and a refresh that fell due while the screen was off used to be
  applied the instant the screen came back — the whole change of cloud cover landing in a single
  frame. Half a sky of cloud could appear at once, with the light changing at the same moment: an
  obvious jolt a second after you unlocked the phone. Now the sky travels to the new weather
  instead of jumping to it, over a few seconds, and each cloud thickens into view rather than
  appearing. A big change takes longer than a small one; a change so small you would not have
  noticed still passes unnoticed.

  Nothing is delayed and no weather is ignored — the reading is the same and arrives at the same
  moment, it is only drawn as a transition. **A freshly started wallpaper does not fade in**: it
  draws the sky it was handed on the first frame, as it always has. With Live Weather off, nothing
  about this changes for you at all.

Nothing you have set is affected. Your themes, colours, sliders and saved themes are exactly as you
left them, and there are no new settings.

**Still the case since v5.3:** this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators,
Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
