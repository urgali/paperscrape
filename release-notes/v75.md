# PaperScrape v75

**Two fixes to the artwork itself, and a lighter set of drawings behind it.**

This stable release gathers everything from the v74.1 and v74.2 betas. The scene
is laid out exactly as before — same objects, same positions, same animations,
same settings, same themes.

## Dolphins and sailboats get their colours back

Turn a lake on and you would see shapes drifting across the water, but they were
blank white silhouettes rather than a dolphin and a sailboat. They now appear in
the colours they were always meant to have: a soft grey-blue dolphin, and a
sailboat with a warm hull under a cream sail.

The drawings themselves were never wrong. They were made the way every
recolourable object in the scene is made — as a plain light shape that the
wallpaper colours in as it draws it — but the drawing code had been told they
already carried their own colours, so it left them exactly as they were. Plain
light shapes drawn without a colour come out white.

## A lighter, tidier set of artwork

Ten pictures were being stored twice. The small and large houses were drawn with
the same window and the same flower planter, kept as separate copies, and each
walking figure's four-frame stride stored its middle pose twice over. The
duplicates have been removed and everything now shares the one drawing, so the
wallpaper loads a little over a megabyte less artwork and has ten fewer pictures
to keep track of while drawing.

## One gap now written down

People walking along the pavement change with the season — a winter hat instead
of hair, longer sleeves, trousers instead of a skirt — but the faces at house
windows and behind car windscreens were never given their winter versions, so
they look the same all year. That artwork still needs to be drawn. It is not
invented here, and the app will now flag it the moment it arrives.

## Still known

The hot-air balloon's basket draws white, for the same reason the dolphin did.
It is recorded and will be fixed in a future release.
