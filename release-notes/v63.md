🎨 Improved (aesthetic pass, corrected)
- Dolphins now actually look like dolphins (rounded body, fin, tail, eye) instead of a plain
  circle.
- Sailboats have a proper big sail on a tall mast instead of a tiny, undefined shape.
- The lake now has moving light/dark water bands and sparkles instead of a flat color.
- Birds are now a proper rounded wing shape instead of two straight lines.
- Santa and the reindeer got more detail (fluffy antlers, red nose, bigger fluffy beard, a
  chunkier sleigh with visible gifts).
- Houses, buildings, and cars are simpler and bolder again — the extra detail added last update
  didn't actually look better, so it's been pulled back out in favor of matching the classic
  flat, bold paper-cutout style more closely.
- The town/scene should feel a bit less crowded by default — houses, buildings, umbrellas, and
  trees now start at a more open density (you can still turn this up or down per category).

🆕 New
- 🎅 Show Santa: a real on/off toggle for Santa's sleigh (Seasonal Decorations > Christmas) —
  previously always on for the Christmas theme with no way to turn it off.
