# v73.7

Fixes the sun's rays and the stars being drawn at the wrong size and in the wrong
place.

## What was wrong

The sun is drawn as two pieces: the disc, and a ring of eight rays behind it. The
rays were being drawn three times too large and hanging off the sun's lower right
rather than sitting around it, so instead of a sunburst you got a scattering of
strokes next to the sun.

The stars had the same problem for the same reason. Each twinkle was three times
too big and pushed down and to the right of the star it belonged to, so the night
sky was coarser and more cluttered than it was meant to be.

Both come from a single detail: a sprite can be drawn either at its literal pixel
size or at three times that, and a PNG carries no record of which it is. In v73
both files were redrawn at the larger size, and the code that places them was
never told.

## What changed

The rays now ring the disc, and the stars are drawn at the size they were before
v73 — small, centred on their own point, and finer against the sky.

The artwork itself was already correct and has not been touched. Nothing else in
the scene changes: the sun and moon still stay on screen and still follow your
swipe exactly as in v73.6, and background scrolling is untouched.

## Also in this release

No new settings and nothing to do after updating. 235 automated tests pass, 7 of
them new: they read the artwork files directly and check that each one's size, its
declared scale and its position agree — the check that was missing when this broke.
