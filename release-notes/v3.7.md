# v3.7

**A different second weather service, a snow cap that was in the wrong place, and a lot of checking
that changed nothing.**

Most of this release is verification. Three of the things looked at turned out not to be problems,
and saying so is the result — nothing was adjusted by eye to make it feel better.

---

## Live Weather: WeatherAPI.com replaces Visual Crossing

**Open-Meteo is still the default and still needs no account.** If you have never touched the
weather provider setting, nothing about this release changes what PaperScrape does.

The optional second service is now **WeatherAPI.com** instead of Visual Crossing. A free account
needs an email address and **no payment card**, and gives 100 000 lookups a month — far more than
one hourly refresh uses. The key is yours, it is stored only on your device, and it is sent only to
WeatherAPI.com.

Two reasons for the change. The first is that the alternative considered, OpenWeather, now requires
a credit card on file even to use its free allowance, which is not a reasonable thing to ask.
The second is duller and matters more: WeatherAPI publishes the full list of its weather codes as a
file a program can read, so PaperScrape's translation of "code 1219" into "moderate snow" is checked
automatically against the service's own list, every time the tests run. The service it replaces
published its equivalent only as prose, and that translation was never checkable by anything.

**If you had chosen Visual Crossing**, PaperScrape now uses Open-Meteo. Nothing breaks and nothing
needs setting up: Open-Meteo needs no key, so it simply works. Your location and every other weather
setting are untouched.

## The road is not too narrow

Somebody looked at the scene and thought the cars might be too big for the road. They are not, and
this was measured rather than eyeballed: the two traffic lanes sit about one car-height apart, which
is exactly what the road was designed for, and the tarmac is more than twice as deep as a car is
tall. Even the fire engine — the largest vehicle in the scene — fits inside it.

What probably reads as "narrow" is that a car in the *far* lane has its roof above the top edge of
the tarmac, because a car stands on its wheels and rises from there. That is what standing on a road
looks like from this angle. Making it go away would mean widening the tarmac until the road took
over the picture, which is a change that was already tried and reverted years ago.

**Nothing was changed.** The measurements are now written into a test, so if the road or the cars
ever do drift out of proportion, it will fail rather than wait to be noticed.

## The winter tree's snow cap, in the theme previews

In the small theme pictures in the gallery, the snow on a winter tree sat three pixels to the right
and two down from where the wallpaper itself draws it. Nobody would have spotted it, but the preview
is supposed to show you what you are about to get.

The two are now built from one description instead of two copies of the same numbers, so they cannot
drift apart again. **The wallpaper is unchanged** — every one of the app's seventeen reference
frames still matches exactly. It was the preview that moved.

## Better detection of graphics regressions

PaperScrape checks its own drawing by rendering reference frames and comparing them pixel by pixel.
There was a known hole in that: the sun's glow is drawn faintly over a bright sky, so it was possible
to break its shape completely without moving enough pixels for a whole-picture comparison to notice.

The check now also looks at the glow on its own, rather than only as part of the whole frame. Two
deliberately broken versions of the glow — one with its shape destroyed, one at half strength — are
now both caught, and both used to pass. The same frame rendered by two completely different graphics
drivers still passes, which is the other half of the requirement: a test that cried wolf on every
different device would be worse than none.

## Smaller things

- The walking-person icon in **World & scene** now uses the current Material version of that icon.
  It looks the same; it will mirror correctly if PaperScrape is ever translated into a
  right-to-left language.
- The app's internal architecture document was badly out of date and has been rewritten against the
  actual code. This changes nothing you can see and everything for whoever works on it next.
- The memory behaviour of the app's internal caches was reviewed. Nothing needed changing, and the
  measurements are recorded so the question does not get reopened on a hunch.
