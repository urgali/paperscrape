## A lighter, tidier set of artwork

The wallpaper looks exactly the same as before. Every scene, object, colour,
animation and setting is unchanged — this release is about the drawings behind
them.

Ten pictures were being stored twice. The small and large houses were drawn with
the same window and the same flower planter, kept as separate copies, and each
walking figure's four-frame stride stored its middle pose twice over. The
duplicates have been removed and everything now shares the one drawing, so the
wallpaper loads a little over a megabyte less artwork and has ten fewer pictures
to keep track of while drawing.

One known gap is now written down rather than left to be rediscovered. People
walking along the pavement change with the season — a winter hat instead of hair,
longer sleeves, trousers instead of a skirt — but the faces at house windows and
behind car windscreens were never given their winter versions, so they look the
same all year. That artwork still needs to be drawn. It is not invented here, and
the app will now flag it the moment it arrives.
