# v73.6

Fixes the sun, the moon and the stars disappearing when **Scroll background** is
switched on.

## What was wrong

With that setting on, the sky drifts sideways along with the hills. The sun and
moon drifted with it, kept going past the left edge of the screen, and only came
back — all at once, from nowhere — when the drift came round again. At the same
time the stars ran out on the right, leaving a widening band of empty sky behind
them, up to the full width of the screen.

How long it took to show up depended on how long the wallpaper had been on screen
and on the time of day, which is why it could look fine for days and then start
happening. Reinstalling the app reset the drift and hid it again for a while. The
underlying fault had been there since scrolling backgrounds were introduced.

## What changed

The sky holds two different kinds of thing, and they now get different treatment.

**Stars** are a repeating field, so the field simply repeats as it scrolls. There
is no longer a gap on either side, and no moment where the whole sky resets.

**The sun and the moon** are single objects, so instead of scrolling forever they
now move within the room they actually have. They still drift, and they still
follow your finger as you swipe between home screens, but they stay on screen.
Around the middle of the day they move exactly as far as they did before. Close
to sunrise and sunset the sun already sits near the edge of the screen, so there
is less room and it moves less — that is the trade for never losing it.

Nothing at all changes when **Scroll background** is off, which is the default.

## Also in this release

No new settings, no artwork changes, and nothing to do after updating. 228
automated tests pass, 17 of them new and written specifically to make this
particular bug impossible to reintroduce.
