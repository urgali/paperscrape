# v76.5

The whole scene has been rebuilt to one scale.

Until now, every kind of object was sized on its own: houses against houses,
cars against cars, people against people. Nothing tied them together, so a car
could end up taller than the person walking past it and a shop smaller than the
house next door.

Now every object declares how big it really is — a person is 1.75 m, a car
1.55 m, a cottage 5.8 m, a tree 9 m — and the scene works the rest out from a
single perspective. What that changes:

- **People, cars, houses, trees and buildings are finally in proportion** to one
  another, at every distance.
- **The scene has real depth.** Objects at the back are now much smaller than
  objects at the front, instead of everything sitting on nearly the same line.
- **The road has been rebuilt.** Two clearly separated lanes, a wider surface,
  and the far lane now drives a little slower than the near one, the way distance
  makes it look.
- **People walk on a proper pavement in front of the road**, sized and paced by
  how far away they are.
- **Towers stand on the skyline behind the village**; restaurants and bars stand
  among the houses where they belong.
- **The scene now adapts to your screen.** It was tuned for one screen size and
  looked too big or too small on others.
- Dolphins are no longer drawn larger than the sailboats beside them.

Saved custom themes are updated automatically the first time this version runs.

No artwork changed in this release.
