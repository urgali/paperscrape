# v2.12

**A moon that rises into an evening sky, day and night crowds, and a control that actually
controls something.**

**The sky no longer turns back to daylight after sunset.** The moon has always appeared exactly at
sunset — but the colour of everything else jumped straight back to full daytime at that instant and
then took over an hour to darken, which is why the moon looked like it was rising in broad
daylight. Sunset and sunrise are now half-light from both sides, and the scene goes on darkening
from there without a jump.

**Show people has separate day and night crowds.** One slider became two — *Day density* and
*Night density* — so you can have a busy street at noon and an empty one at midnight, or the
reverse. The street fills and empties across dusk and dawn along with the light, rather than
changing between one frame and the next. If you had already set a density, both start at that same
value, so nothing looks different until you move one of them.

**Sun/Cloud Height goes from 0 to 100% like every other slider, and the clouds follow it.** It used
to show an internal number as if it were a percentage, so its "60%" was actually the highest
setting rather than something near the middle. And while it did move the clouds, it moved them by
so little that it looked broken. Now the sun's arc and the cloud band travel together across a
range you can actually see — and at the default setting the sky looks exactly as it did before.

**More room at the bottom of long settings screens.** The last row of World & scene, Theme and
Weather & time could still end up partly under the gesture bar. The app now takes the larger of the
two window measurements available to it instead of trusting one of them, so the end of a page is
reachable whichever one is reporting correctly.
