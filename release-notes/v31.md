✨ New
- Brand new app icon: a little paper-cutout house on a snowy hill, with the sun out.
- Release notes (this text!) now show up right in the app when an update is available,
  not just on GitHub.
- Updated to support Android 16 properly.

🐛 Fixed
- Buildings and houses no longer "float" above the hills or get cut off by the road.
- Snowmen, gifts, penguins, Easter eggs, and other seasonal decorations no longer get
  sliced by the road either.
- Santa's sleigh now flies smoothly instead of moving in little jumps.
- Santa's gifts now fall all the way down to the houses instead of vanishing mid-air.
- Skyscrapers are reliably taller than houses again.

⚠️ Changed
- Dogs have been removed for now — they're getting rebuilt from scratch and will be
  back in a future update.
- Tap sounds and tap reactions have been removed for now, for the same reason.

🔧 Under the hood
- Updated the whole build system (Gradle/Android Gradle Plugin) to their latest stable
  versions.
- Release builds are now properly signed, and every release is published with a
  verifiable build record (see the bottom of this page for how to check it).
