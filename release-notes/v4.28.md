# v4.28

- **The birds read the way they fly.** They were reported as flying backwards, and v4.27 showed
  that they are not — the drawing faces the right way and the app never mirrors it. But the report
  was pointing at something real: at the size a bird actually reaches, the biggest shape in it was
  the raised wing, which rises up and *forward*, and a pair of shapes like that is also what a
  fanned tail looks like. So the eye turned the animal round. The bird has been redrawn as a
  swallow — a deep forked tail and swept-back wings, so the heaviest part of the shape is now
  behind the head, where it belongs. Nothing was flipped, nothing else moved, and the bird is the
  same size in the same place.
- **People put umbrellas up in the rain.** Adults walking in the rain now carry one, in a handful
  of colours drawn from the scene's own palette. Children walk through it as before, and nobody
  carries an umbrella in the snow. An umbrella never appears or disappears in front of you: a
  walker who is already on screen when the rain starts finishes the crossing without one, so what
  you see is people arriving with umbrellas rather than umbrellas blinking into people's hands.
- **The sea moves in bad weather.** When it is raining or there is a thunderstorm, breakers now
  roll across the lake — a wave with a curling lip and spray thrown out in front of it. **In clear
  weather there are none at all**, and the water looks exactly as it did before. The water itself is
  not recoloured: the sky's reflection, the light's path and the boats' wakes are all still there,
  and the shoreline is unchanged.
- **The waves take their colour from the water they are on**, worked out fresh for every frame
  rather than fixed, so they read on a bright noon sea and on a dark one — the same approach that
  fixed the rain in v4.27. On a clear night they are stronger than a plain minimum would make them,
  because a minimum is not the same thing as looking right.
- **A wave in front of a boat is drawn in front of it.** Waves used to be painted underneath
  everything on the water, so a breaker crossing the near edge of the lake was cut off behind a
  boat that was obviously further away. Waves, boats and dolphins are now sorted together by how
  near they are.

**Nothing you have set changes.** No setting was added, removed or renumbered, no saved theme is
touched, and every scene you have built looks like itself. In clear weather the scene is drawn
exactly as v4.27 drew it.
