# v4.20

A tidying release. Nothing new was drawn; what changed is that several things which were supposed
to vary finally do.

- **The three cars actually take turns now.** They were meant to be dealt out evenly and were not:
  the saloon turned up half the time and the compact only one time in five. All three are now close
  to a third each, and no two cars side by side in the two lanes are ever the same model.
- **Not everybody in the traffic is the same person any more.** Because of the way a car picked its
  occupants, **every car on the road was being driven by the same woman**, in every theme, and the
  boy never rode at all. Both adults drive now and all four people ride.
- **People in cars have a second set of clothes.** Until now a passenger's top told you which
  person they were — one colour for the man, one for the woman, every time. Each of them has a
  second outfit now, so the colour above the window sill is no longer just a label. It is a small
  detail by design — the clothing is about four or five pixels tall on screen — but it is the piece
  that used to give the game away.
- **One fire engine, one police car, one taxi.** Two of the same could turn up in the same frame
  more often than the density slider suggested — eight of the twelve themes were carrying a
  duplicate. Now they cannot.
- **Saved themes are repaired when they load.** A theme saved by an older build could contain two
  of the same shop on one tile, or two of the same special vehicle. Both are corrected once, the
  first time such a theme is opened. Your colours, densities and everything you chose are
  untouched.
- Four unused drawings and a handful of duplicate ones were removed from the app.

## One thing worth knowing, which is Android and not the wallpaper

If PaperScrape's process is **force-stopped** — from Settings, from a task killer, or by anything
that hard-kills it — Android does not bring the live wallpaper back. It rebinds with no wallpaper
at all, falls back to the system's static image, and then *saves* that choice, so it survives a
reboot too. You have to set the wallpaper again by hand.

This was investigated on a real device and reproduced deliberately twice: there is no crash and no
error, and there is nothing the app can do about it, because the decision is taken by the system
after the app's process is already gone. It is written down here so that if it happens to you, you
know what it was.
