# PaperScrape v4.11

Three small corrections you can see, and a round of documentation that had drifted away from what
the app actually does. Every setting, theme and custom theme carries over untouched.

## The clouds sit where the weather says they do

Cloud cover has always been decided at a point in the sky, and the cloud picture was then drawn
around it. The two had come apart: the drawing was positioned for a cloud image that no longer
ships, so every cloud in every scene was painted a little above — and a little to the side of — the
place it was supposed to cover.

The offset is now taken from the picture itself, so the drawn cloud and the cloud the scene reasons
about are the same cloud. In practice the sky sits slightly lower and denser weather lands where it
belongs. **This is the only visible difference in the release** — nothing else about the scene has
moved.

## Winter hats got their bobbles back

Five of the winter figures — two of the walkers, the woman at the window and both people riding in
cars — were drawn on a canvas three pixels too short, so the top of a woolly hat was sliced off flat
where the pompom should be. In a snowy theme, with people at the windows and in the traffic, the
missing tops were visible.

The three sets of figures they belong to were each made a little taller together, so every walker,
every face at a window and every driver still lines up with the others exactly as before. Nothing
else about them changed: same size on screen, same position on the pavement, same colours, and all
72 skin-tone variants moved with them.

## Santa's presents land on the pavement

The presents dropped from the sleigh were aimed at a fixed spot that stopped being the pavement
several releases ago, when the road was moved. Some of them landed between the wheel lines. The
target is now read from where the pavement actually is, so they land on it wherever it sits.

## Everything else

The rest of the release is documentation. Some of it mattered: one file told contributors to keep
the drawing code free of OpenGL, which is the renderer the app runs on. The declared height of a few
scene objects has been corrected to match the artwork that was already being drawn — the drawing
itself is unchanged, and the largest difference anywhere on screen is a hundredth of a pixel.

No settings were renamed or removed. Upgrading from v4.10 keeps everything as it is.
