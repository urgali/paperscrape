🎨 Look and feel
- Houses, trees, and palm trees have been redrawn from scratch to look much nicer — rounder,
  softer "paper cutout" shapes instead of plain triangles/rectangles: houses now have a
  rounded roof peak and an arched door, tree canopies are a fuller, bumpier "cloud" shape
  instead of 3 plain circles, and palm trees have a properly curved trunk with bark texture and
  elegant fanned-out fronds instead of straight lines.
- Every one of these objects now has a very subtle soft shading baked in (like a gentle paper
  fold), on top of your chosen color — so colors may look a few percent darker in places than
  the exact color you picked, instead of perfectly flat. Please take a look on a real device and
  let me know if this bothers you at all; it's easy to tone down or remove if so.

⚙️ Under the hood
- This is "batch 1" of a larger aesthetic overhaul: only houses, trees, and palm trees were
  redone this round (the same 4 objects converted to the faster image-based approach last
  update). Everything else — buildings, cars, clouds, mountains, the lake, birds, sun/moon,
  seasonal decorations, and more — is unchanged for now and will get the same treatment in
  future updates, one batch at a time so each can be checked before moving to the next.
- Could not run a full local test build this round (the tool environment didn't have Android's
  build tools available this time) — please treat this update as a bit less proven than usual
  until you've built/installed it yourself and taken a look.
