# v4.31

**Nothing on screen changed, and that is the point.** This is a round of defect work: the
wallpaper draws the same picture it drew in v4.30, pixel for pixel, and what moved is the
machinery that is supposed to notice when it *doesn't*.

- **The check that watches for accidental changes had a blind spot large enough to hide a bird.**
  The wallpaper is tested by rendering fixed scenes and comparing them against saved pictures, and
  that comparison used to forgive up to 576 pixels of a frame. In v4.28 the bird was redrawn; six
  of the saved pictures were never updated with the new one, and because a bird is smaller than
  576 pixels, nothing complained. It stayed that way for two releases.

  The comparison now forgives **nothing**: a scene that renders differently from its saved picture
  fails, full stop. That is not a stricter rule invented for its own sake — on the test phone a
  matching scene has always come out identical to the last pixel, so zero is simply what the
  measurement actually reads.

- **Three of the boats-and-dolphins drawings were carrying invisible blank margins**, left behind
  when the lake was redrawn in v4.26. They are trimmed, with the drawing positions adjusted by
  exactly as much as was trimmed, so every boat and every dolphin is on the same pixel it was on
  before. The tool that does this trimming had two faults of its own — it could never finish, and
  it gave up half way through after already changing files — and both are fixed.

- **Two of the project's own checks had been failing for four releases** because nothing in the
  release routine ran them. They pass, and the routine now runs them.

- **A handful of comments that explained a number were describing a version of the drawing that
  no longer exists** — including one that said a dolphin sits exactly on the point it leaps from,
  when it has been a whisker off since v4.26. The comments are corrected. Whether to *move* the
  dolphin is a question about the picture, and it is left for a decision rather than taken
  quietly here.

- **The rain is unchanged, and a limit that guards it had quietly loosened.** When the children
  were redrawn shorter in v4.30, a rule saying "a raindrop must never get so small it disappears"
  was measured against the children — so it relaxed by 16 % on its own. It is now measured against
  an adult, at exactly the length it has always meant.

No new artwork, no new settings, nothing to re-choose.
