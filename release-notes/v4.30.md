# v4.30

**The people are different.** They are dealt fresh colours every time they walk across, the
children look like children, and behind that the wallpaper stopped shipping a separate drawing for
every colour a person can be.

- **The same person does not walk past twice in the same clothes.** Skin, hair or hat, shirt and
  trousers are chosen anew each time a walker crosses the screen — and so is the colour of the
  umbrella, which used to be the one thing on the street that never changed.

  What was wrong before was not that the choice was unfair, it was that it was made **once per
  theme and then never again**. On *Big City* that meant three men who were all fair-skinned and
  three children who were all dark-skinned, for as long as you left the theme on. Now the street
  re-deals itself as it goes.

  **The change never happens in front of you.** A walker's colours can only move while no copy of
  that figure is anywhere on screen — the same rule that already governs who is carrying an
  umbrella when it starts to rain. And the deal is driven by the clock, so it keeps moving across
  the day instead of starting again from the same hand every time the wallpaper restarts.

- **Children look like children.** They were drawn at 78 % of an adult, which is the proportion of
  a ten-year-old, and worse, they had an adult's proportions: the same share of leg, the same
  shape, just smaller. They are now **65 %**, with a child's build — the head barely smaller than
  an adult's and the height taken out of the legs and the body, which is what actually changes as
  a person grows. The stride and the arm swing shortened with the legs, so a child no longer
  marches like a grown-up.

  The children in cars and at windows are untouched and still match.

- **A person is now drawn, not shipped in every colour.** Until this release every figure was
  included once per skin tone: 168 drawings that were the same picture with one colour moved.
  A figure is now a single drawing plus four small maps saying which parts of it follow which
  colour, and the colours are applied as it is drawn.

  That is what made the rest of this release possible. Adding hair, shirt and trouser colours the
  old way would have meant 27 copies of every person; this way they cost nothing extra at all, and
  the next colour after them will cost nothing either.

- **It costs no extra work per frame.** Drawing a figure in layers sounds like drawing it several
  times, and on some designs it would be. Measured on the test phone, a crowded street is still
  **one drawing command per frame** — exactly what it was before — because the layers are gathered
  into the same batch as everything else. The graphics memory the wallpaper uses went **down**, by
  19 %.

- **Twelve drawings that could never appear have been removed.** A person at a window is indoors,
  so they are never drawn in winter clothing — a deliberate rule, not an oversight. The winter
  versions were being shipped anyway and nothing could ever show them.

**Nothing you have set up changes.** No setting was added, removed or renumbered, no saved theme is
touched, and nothing outside the pavement, the road and the windows is drawn differently.
