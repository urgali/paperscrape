🐛 Fixed
- "Reset everything to defaults" in Scene Objects actually works now —
  it used to silently do nothing if you'd ever saved changes to that
  theme before.
- "Scroll background" now does what it was always supposed to: the
  scenery gently drifts on its own, all the time, not just when you
  swipe between home screens. New "Scroll speed" slider controls how
  fast.
- Updating the app now shows you everything that changed since your
  version, not just the newest release's notes — and the list scrolls
  properly if it's long.
- Clouds no longer look sparse even at 100% — there are more of them now,
  and they get bigger too as you turn the slider up.
- Fresh installs now actually look different theme to theme: Beach has
  a sea with sailboats and dolphins, Christmas/Winter/Tundra have
  snow-capped mountains, Desert has sandy mountains and no water.

This is a first, quick pass on theme defaults — a fuller review is still
coming.
