# v4.27

- **The rain can be seen again.** Rain was painted in one colour taken from your theme, while the
  sky behind it changed all day — and at some hour of the day, in eleven of the twelve built-in
  themes, the two ended up exactly the same brightness. When that happened the drops simply were
  not there until they fell far enough to reach the hills, which is what was reported. The rain now
  keeps a minimum distance from whatever sky it is falling through: it is still the colour you
  chose, held just far enough from the sky to be visible, and in a theme where it already stood out
  nothing changes at all. The distance is not a guess — it was measured across every theme, every
  hour and all three kinds of weather, and it is set between the case where the rain vanished and
  the case where it reads.
- **Snow was measured the same way and left alone**, because it does not have the problem: even at
  its worst, falling snow is far brighter than any sky in the app.
- **A lake under the Halloween sky is the right colour now.** With the horror sky switched on, the
  water was reflecting a colour that had never been worked out — on the first frame, transparent
  black. You only saw it if you turned the lake on with that sky, which is why it went unnoticed.

**About the birds.** They were reported as flying backwards — tail first, head behind. They are
not: the drawing already faces the way they fly, and the app never mirrors it. What is true is that
at the size a bird is drawn on screen the head is about three pixels and the raised wing is the
biggest shape in it, so which way the bird is going is hard to read. Nothing was changed to the
drawing in this release, because changing it is a decision about how the scene looks rather than a
bug to fix. There is now a reference frame with a bird in it, so no future change to that drawing
can go out without somebody having looked at it.

**Nothing you have set changes.** No setting was added, removed or renumbered, no saved theme is
touched, and every scene you have built looks like itself.
