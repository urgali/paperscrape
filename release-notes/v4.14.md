# PaperScrape v4.14

The automatic night colours go darker again — the last pass fixed white and left everything else
behind — and the birds stop fading out during the golden hour.

## Night colours, checked against more than one colour

v4.13 rebuilt the day/night rule and settled its numbers on a single sample: a near-white hill on
the Christmas theme. That colour came out right and the rest did not. Red houses in particular were
still noticeably too red after dark.

The numbers have been set again, this time against a spread of eleven surface kinds — snow, cream,
foliage, a saturated red, brick, glass, water, asphalt and both achromatic ends — and against the
range the twelve built-in themes use for their own hand-authored night colours. Everything now lands
inside that range instead of just above it:

- a bright red house becomes a **deep maroon**, still a red house, no longer a lit one;
- snow-white hills reach a proper night tone rather than a pale grey;
- colours keep their hue and lose saturation, which is what colour does when the light goes.

Checked on a phone: three objects set to three different daylight colours, each producing exactly
the night colour the rule predicts, and the scene rendering the way the swatches say it should.

## Birds no longer fade out at sunset

With "night birds" turned off, the flock was supposed to be gone after dark. What it actually did
was start disappearing well before then: birds were fading from about an hour and a half before
sunset and were **half transparent at sunset itself**, with the sky visible through them — during
the part of the evening they are most worth looking at.

They are now solid for the whole time the sun is up, and leave over about half an hour once it has
set. Nothing changes if you have night birds switched on.

## Weather and time, quieter fixes

- The sunrise and sunset times are recalculated when the day changes or the clock moves to summer
  time. Before, they were worked out once per location fix and could drift for weeks.
- Refresh scheduling no longer uses the wall clock, so moving the clock backwards — by hand or by a
  time-zone change — can no longer pause weather updates for hours.
- The wallpaper preview no longer overwrites the live wallpaper's weather status with its own.

## Privacy

- The app's list of the internet addresses it contacts is now complete. The city-search endpoint was
  missing from it, and only one of the three weather providers was named.
- Coordinates sent to a weather provider are rounded to about a kilometre. The forecast is the same
  — the providers' own grids are far coarser than that — but the request no longer carries a
  precise position.
- An API key you paste in is now escaped properly, so a key containing punctuation works instead of
  being silently rejected.
