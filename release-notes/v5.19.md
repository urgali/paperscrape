This release brings the whole town below the road and lets every switch be turned on on every theme. It also brings a switch for the fireworks, a road that can stay with no cars on it, weather that follows you when you move, and a Sun/Cloud Height slider that keeps the sun and the moon on the screen.

If you have 5.16 on Android 9 or 10: the update button in the app cannot install 5.19 there, because the fault is in the 5.16 that does the installing. Download 5.19 from the release page on GitHub and install it from there, once. From 5.17 on, updating from the app works on Android 9 and 10 too.

The town below the road

- The row below the road now shows the street twice, with the whole town: the restaurant, the bar, the shop, the school, the fire station and the police station, the houses and the trees, with the same drawings as the street above. A shop or public building below the road is never the same as one standing above it on the screen, nor as the one next to it. The towers stay on the street behind. The row follows the switches and amounts you already have.

Every switch on every theme

- No switch is greyed because of the theme any more. On saved, imported and random themes some switches used to be greyed with "This theme has no ..."; now every theme has houses, trees, parasols, towers, shops and public buildings, and cars, and every switch can be turned on. On a random theme what it was born without, apart from the trees, starts off, so it looks as it did until you turn it on; on a saved theme what was off stays off. The streets of the twelve themes that come with the app do not change.

- A new switch, Show shops and public buildings, on the Towers & offices page: the restaurant, the bar, the shop, the school, the fire station and the police station among the houses, below the road too. It is on as themes come, and off on a random theme born without them. On the same page, the line about the Towers slider now stands right under the slider.

- On a random theme, Show Trees, Show Houses and Show Parasols on, at the amount the theme comes with, now always show some along the street. A random theme whose own trees, houses or parasols would all be thinned out at that amount is given more of them, beside its own.

- Fireworks can be turned on on every theme: a Fireworks switch on a new New Year's Eve page of Seasons & decorations, "Fireworks burst over the town at night." It is on for New Year's Eve and for the random themes that came with fireworks, and off on the others; a saved theme keeps what it had.

Cars and the road

- At 0 % no car drives at that hour; before, one did. With both car amounts at 0 %, Show Cars reads off and says so.

- Keep the road, under Show Cars: with no car at any hour, it keeps the road, empty, or takes it away so the meadow runs to the houses. It is off as themes come. While cars drive at some hour, the road is there all day.

Live Weather

- The weather is asked for again as soon as the place changes: a new custom location at once, and your phone's position once it has moved more than 3 km. The position is the one the phone already knows, read for free without starting the GPS for it, so it is new at once only when another app, such as a map, has asked for it; otherwise the weather follows within the hour, as before. The lines under Live Weather, GPS and Network say so.

The sun and the clouds

- The Sun/Cloud Height slider now goes up to where the sun stays whole on the screen, under the status bar; before, above about 70 % the sun and the moon began to go off the top. The clouds come down with the sun and stay under it, and the Halloween moon that stays in place comes down with the clouds. So at every height but 0 %, the one each theme comes with included, the sun, the moon and the clouds stand lower than before; a height you had set keeps its number.

Building

- The tools that build the app are up to date (Android Gradle Plugin 9.4.1, Gradle 9.8.2, Kotlin 2.4.21), and so are the app's libraries (Compose 2026.09.00, core-ktx 1.19.1, DataStore 1.3.0); the app needs no change for Android 17.

The work for your phone

- On our test phone the wallpaper uses a little more of the processor than 5.18, for the whole town below the road: about 1 to 2 points of one core more, from 2 % to 5 % more, on an autumn noon and night, a Christmas night with everything on and Big City at noon, and 3.5 points more (8 %) on a Halloween night in heavy rain, with about the same memory. On the Christmas night with everything on a frame comes a moment late about one time in 11 instead of one in 17, and in the Halloween heavy rain one time in 20 instead of one in 48; in the other scenes about as often as before.

If you are updating from 5.18:

- Below the road you see the whole town.
- The sun, the moon and the clouds stand lower, at the height each theme comes with and at any you had set, unless it was 0 %.
- If you had Show Cars on with both car amounts at 0 %, no car drives, Show Cars reads off and the road stays, empty.
- On a random theme whose own trees, houses or parasols were all thinned out, with Show Trees, Show Houses or Show Parasols on, some now stand along the street.
- On a random theme born without houses, or without shops and public buildings, their switch now reads off, and the theme's card in the gallery no longer shows them.
- Nothing else appears by itself: what a random or saved theme did not have stays off until you turn it on.

Nothing else you have set has changed: your themes, colours and sliders are as you left them.

Still the case since v5.3: this version does not install on Intel-based devices or emulators, Chromebooks running Android apps included. v5.2 remains the last version that does.
