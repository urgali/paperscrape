# v2.6

Two corrections to what v2.5 introduced.

**The outline is now on the outside.** v2.5 tucked a fine edge *inside* each shape, which
looked right standing still and came apart in motion: on a walking figure the band appeared
and vanished between frames as the arms and legs moved. Every sprite now carries a real
outline drawn around its whole silhouette instead — one continuous band of one thickness,
following the actual shape, and it no longer changes from frame to frame. It is still thin
and still in the subject's own tone rather than a black cartoon outline.

**Small houses are wider.** They had the right height but not enough facade, so the two
windows sat almost on the edges and looked ready to fall off the front. The wall and roof are
noticeably wider now, with a proper margin of wall either side of each window. The height, the
roof pitch, the centred door and the pair of windows are all unchanged.

Nothing else moved. The world scale, the Spring theme, the Halloween palms and moon, the
dolphins and the automatic calendar are exactly as they were in v2.5.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with v1.0,
so Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears your saved
settings and custom themes.
