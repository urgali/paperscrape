# v5.0

**The town is redrawn.** Every house, tower, restaurant and bar in the wallpaper is a new drawing,
and the biggest change is not any one of them — it is that they are no longer all the same.

**That is why the number jumped to 5.0.** The buildings have been redrawn before, a few at a time;
this time they were all started again from a blank sheet, in a different style, and the street you
wake up to will not be the one you went to sleep with. Nothing you have set is affected — the whole
of the change is on the screen — but a town you have to look at twice is worth a whole number.

- **No two houses on the street are alike any more.** A house is now built up out of pieces — a
  ground floor, none or one or two upper storeys, and a roof — and which pieces each house gets is
  decided by where it stands. So one neighbour has a gable and one has a mansard, one is two
  storeys and the one beside it is three, and a big house may carry a little turret. It is the same
  house every time you look at it: the choice is fixed by the building's position, not re-rolled,
  so your street stays your street.

- **The tower, the restaurant and the bar are new drawings too.** The tower steps back twice on its
  way up and wears either a spire or a dome; the restaurant is a pavilion with a domed sign and a
  striped awning; the bar comes in two shapes, one with a signboard and one with a cut-off corner.

- **The two shops are lower than they were.** They used to be two-storey buildings; they are
  single-storey now, so they sit below the houses instead of level with them. This is the drawing
  that was chosen, not something that slipped — but it is the most visible change on a street after
  the houses themselves, so it is worth knowing before you look for it.

- **Your colours still do everything.** The eight colours you can edit — four for houses, four for
  the other buildings — are still the whole palette. Every roof, cornice, door and band on the new
  buildings is your own colour taken lighter or darker, so moving one slider moves the whole town
  with it, the way it always did.

- **Everything the buildings used to do, they still do**: people appear at the windows, the lights
  come on in the evening, shops go dark at closing time while homes do not, snow settles on the
  roofs in winter, and Christmas lights hang from the sills.

  One number did change with the drawing: a tower has three windows somebody can be seen in, where
  the old one had sixteen small ones. The new tower's other windows are too small to hold a figure.

- **The leaping dolphin now leaps from where it appears to.** It has been landing a fraction of its
  own body off the point its arc is drawn around since the lake was redrawn; it is on it now.

**One thing that is not fixed, and is worth saying.** If you push a category's colour to an extreme
— almost white, or almost black — some of the details stop separating from the wall: on a very pale
house the window frames and the coping disappear into it, and on a very dark one the roof and the
door do. This is not new; the buildings that shipped before did it too, and it was measured on both
before the new ones were accepted. Correcting it would cost more than it is worth in artwork, so it
stays, and it is written down rather than left to be found.

No new settings, and nothing to re-choose: your themes, colours and sliders are exactly as you left
them.
