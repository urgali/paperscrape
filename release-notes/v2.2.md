# v2.2

**The wallpaper looks exactly the same, and uses noticeably less memory.**

Most of the artwork was drawn on a canvas larger than the drawing itself, leaving a
border of empty space around each sprite. That space was decoded, stored and drawn
every frame, and it never showed anything. It has been removed: the sprite set now
decodes to 14.79 MB instead of 16.28 MB, a saving of about 1.5 MB, with a
correspondingly smaller texture atlas on the GPU.

Trimming a sprite moves its own frame of reference, so every place the app draws one
had to be adjusted by exactly the amount trimmed, in the same change. That was done
for all 34 sprites and groups affected, and the result was checked pixel by pixel:
every visible pixel of every sprite is the same colour, in the same place, as it was
before. Nothing in the scene moves.

A small number of sprites were deliberately left as they are — the sun, the moon
phases, the stars and the fireworks. Those are positioned by the centre of their
canvas rather than by their drawing, so trimming them would mean changing how they
are placed rather than just how much space they take. That is not worth doing for a
few kilobytes.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1
with v1.0, so Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first —
which clears your saved settings and custom themes.
