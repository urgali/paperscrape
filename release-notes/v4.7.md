# PaperScrape v4.7

Santa's sleigh got redrawn.

## The whole team, not just the driver

Every December the sleigh crosses the sky a few times an hour, and until now it was the one thing
in the scene that had never had a second pass. The reindeer were an oval with a wedge for a neck,
the sleigh was a plank on two runners, and the sack was a brown ellipse with two boxes balanced on
top of it.

All of it is new.

**The reindeer** are drawn as one animal now instead of a few shapes that happened to touch:
a proper barrel, a neck that reads as a neck, a lighter muzzle, thicker antlers that survive being
small, and a red collar with a gold bell — the same warm cream-and-gold the rest of the scene uses.

**The sleigh** is a sleigh. It has a curved prow, runners that curl up at the front and hook up at
the back, a scrolled back that rises behind Santa's shoulders, and a body that sits on its runners
instead of floating above them.

**Santa is sitting in it.** He always looked like he was pinned behind a board, because the sleigh's
side ran behind him. Now that side is drawn in front of his lower half and his mitten rests on the
edge, which is all it takes to read as "in" rather than "on".

**The sack** is a tied cloth sack in deep red, cinched at the neck with a cord, with presents
showing from behind it rather than stacked on top.

Santa's own face, beard, hat and coat are exactly as they were. Nothing else in any theme changed,
and the sleigh flies at the same size, on the same path, at the same times as before.
