# v76.11

- **People walk with the world.** Swiping between home screens used to slide the
  village past them while they stayed put. They now scroll with the ground they are
  standing on, and their walk reads as walking rather than sliding.
- **Live Weather tells you when it can't find you.** If your location isn't
  available, the scene keeps running on the theme's own weather — as it always did —
  and the settings screen now says so instead of leaving the switch looking dead. The
  message appears and clears on its own.
- **Update checks are opt-in.** PaperScrape no longer contacts GitHub every time you
  open the settings. There's a switch for it, and the manual check button always
  works.
- **Dolphins and boats keep out of each other's way.** They can no longer end up in
  the same lane.

Snow on rooftops and people density controls are still to come.
