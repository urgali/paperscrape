🐛 Fixed — mountains
- Mountains from the last update were rendering as basically invisible (just two thin curved
  slivers, with the rest of the shape showing empty sky/background instead of solid mountain).
  A math mistake in the new two-tone shading — fixed, mountains are solid again.

🐛 Fixed — dolphins and sailboats
- Dolphins and sailboats were almost never visible on the lake — they were swimming in a part of
  the water that the hills in front usually cover up. Moved them to swim closer to the top of
  the lake (the part that's actually visible), so they should show up much more often now —
  especially on the Big City theme, where they should now always be visible.
