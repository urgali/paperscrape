# v2.4

A refinement pass over what v2.3 introduced, plus a Halloween theme to put it in.

**A Halloween theme.** The eleventh built-in. Choosing it shows the whole presentation at
once — the carved moon, the bare trees, the black-and-orange sky, pumpkins on the ground —
without hunting for switches in a menu. Both switches are still yours afterwards: turn
either off, in any combination, and neither one drags the other with it.

**A scarier moon.** The carved face was legible but friendly. It now has narrow slanted
eyes with the inner corner dropped, and a wide ragged grin with uneven fangs above and
below. Still cut clean out of the disc so the sky shows through it, and still readable at
the size a moon is actually drawn.

**The birds finally look like birds.** The wings had small notches underneath that read as
claws, a hard elbow in the leading edge, and a head sitting apart from the body — three
things that together said bat. They are gulls now: long tapered wings drawn to a point, a
body and head in one piece, a tail that narrows away.

**The dolphins are rounder and better shaped**, with a domed forehead, a proper beak and a
swept fin, built from fewer, larger pieces.

**Dolphins now splash both ways.** They already threw water on the way back in. They now
break the surface with one too, and the animal rises up through it. Each burst fires once
per crossing and fades in about a tenth of a second — no trail following them across the
lake.

Nothing else changed. Saved themes and custom colours are untouched, and a theme saved
before this release loads exactly as it did.

**If you are coming from a pre-release test build:** version numbers restarted at 1 with
v1.0, so Android will not install this over one of those. Uninstall first — which clears
your saved settings and custom themes.
