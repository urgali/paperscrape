☀️ Look and feel — sun redrawn
- The sun now has the same soft "paper cutout" texture as everything else, instead of being a
  plain flat-colored circle.

🌙 Look and feel — the moon now shows its real phase shape
- Moon phases (crescent, quarter, gibbous, full, and back down) now use real distinct shapes
  instead of a mathematical approximation — closer to how the moon actually looks night to
  night if you have realistic phases turned on.

✨ Look and feel — stars redrawn
- Stars are now small 4-pointed sparkles instead of plain dots, and a bit bigger so the shape
  actually shows. They also slowly rotate for a bit of twinkle.

🐦 Look and feel — birds redrawn
- Birds have a redrawn, softer silhouette, and their wing-flap animation now flips between two
  positions (matching how the original inspiration handles it) instead of continuously bending.
