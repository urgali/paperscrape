✨ New
- New "Hills" colors: pick your own Day Color and Night Color for the
  hills, per theme.
- New "Mountains" section: two independent background layers (front and
  back), each with its own show/hide, colors, and how many appear.
- New "Lake" section: add a body of water to any theme, with its own
  colors, height, and optional sailboats and dolphins drifting across it.
- New "Birds" section: a flock of birds flying across the sky, with 4
  customizable colors (and how often each one shows up), and an option
  to let them fly at night too.
- New "Scroll background" setting: the sun, moon, and stars can now
  optionally scroll along with the landscape when you swipe between home
  screens (previously only the ground and buildings moved).
- New "Swipe scroll" setting: turn off wallpaper scrolling entirely if
  you'd rather it stay still while you swipe.
- "Parallax strength" is now also labeled "Scroll speed" — same setting,
  clearer name.

🐛 Fixed
- Buildings and houses no longer drift out of sync with the hills while
  swiping between home screens — they used to slowly slip apart from the
  terrain the more you scrolled.

All of the above (except the two Scroll settings, which are general) work
exactly like the rest of Scene Objects: per theme, freely editable, and
saveable over the built-in theme or as your own custom one.
