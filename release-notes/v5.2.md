# v5.2

**A small release with one visible fix.** On the Desert theme, the bar that stands beside the big
house is back where it belongs. If you do not use the Desert theme, this update changes nothing you
can see — and it is worth saying that plainly rather than dressing it up.

- **A shop was being pushed down the street by a palm tree that was not really in its way.** When
  the wallpaper lays out a street it checks whether anything would stand in front of a shop, and
  moves the shop along until its front is clear — shops are the one thing in the scene you are
  meant to be able to read. To do that it needs to know how much room each tree takes up, and a
  palm crown was being treated as a solid block the full size of its picture. A palm crown is not
  a solid block: it is seven separate blades with sky between them, and more than half of that
  picture is empty. So the bar kept being moved out from behind a crown that was mostly gaps, and
  ended up sixteen slots further along the street than the layout had put it.

  The wallpaper now measures how much of each tree is actually drawn, instead of assuming the
  whole picture is. The Desert bar sits beside the big house again, its window, door, lamp and the
  figure at the window all readable, with the palm fronds passing over its roof.

- **Nothing else moved.** This was checked rather than assumed: the other eleven themes lay their
  streets out exactly as they did in v5.1, down to the pixel. Trees whose shape is solid — an oak's
  crown, a parasol — take up exactly as much room as before, because for those the new measurement
  and the old assumption give the same answer. Only a crown made of separate blades changes.

Nothing you have set is affected. Your themes, colours, sliders and saved themes are exactly as you
left them, and there are no new settings.
